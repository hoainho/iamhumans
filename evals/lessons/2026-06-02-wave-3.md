## What Wave 3 confirmed

1. **ATT module is scoring above baseline** — 97.3 mean vs. 96.54 corpus baseline. Early-wounding and relational attachment territory is handled cleanly without clinical labels.
2. **Somatic cases (SOM module) are the strongest** — SOM cluster (TC-328–TC-352) averaged ~97.5, with multiple 100s. Short somatic-register replies match the rubric well.
3. **Cross-module cases hold** — the 10 cross-module cases (ATT×SOM, ATT×RD) averaged 96.5, no hard fails. The modules are compatible in combination.
4. **Identity/self closing arc (TC-398–TC-401) passed cleanly** — the "container," "turned off," and "fogged glass" imagery was received rather than replaced; closing gratitude (TC-401) was accepted without deflection or AI-disclosure frame-break.
5. **Stylistic floor is stable** — `naturalness` 9.25, `empathy_fit` 9.35 unchanged. This is a design constraint, not a regression. Reaching 9.5+ would require responses that are consistently indistinguishable from a senior therapist's prose — achievable but requiring per-case tuning beyond book-grounded rules.

## What Wave 4 should address

### Priority 1 — Raise `naturalness` floor
The 9.25 naturalness average reflects responses that are clean but occasionally show micro-tells: slightly formulaic openers, minor triplet structuring, near-identical sentence rhythm across cases. A voice-tuning pass (no new corpus rules) could target the 15–20 cases scoring 9 on naturalness and identify the specific tells.

### Priority 2 — TC-098 persistent floor
TC-098 (88/100) — user references prior context the model doesn't have — is the only case consistently below 90. The one-sentence cap rule (v2.6.1) has not pushed it above 90. Consider: a micro-rewrite of the specific template sentence, or a new test variant to identify the exact failing form.

### Priority 3 — Wave 4 module selection
Candidates by coverage gap:
- **CON (Coercion/Power)** — Bancroft depth, Herman, Walker, Lundy. High-risk territory (stay/leave verdicts must be avoided). Distinct from RD module. ~50 rules projected.
- **GRF-X (Extended Grief)** — Klass, Stroebe, Neimeyer, Boss (ambiguous loss). Wave 1 GRF module covered basics; this extends into anticipatory grief, disenfranchised grief, continuing bonds.
- **ILL (Illness/Body)** — Frank, Kleinman depth, Reynolds Price. Chronic illness, disability, diagnosis. Somatic register but distinctly different from SOM.

**Recommended Wave 4: CON** — highest behavioral risk if untrained, most distinct from existing modules, strongest source corpus available.
