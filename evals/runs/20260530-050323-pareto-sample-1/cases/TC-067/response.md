Nothing's wrong with you. That's actually how grief mostly works — a short stretch where it floods everything, then it just... sets itself down somewhere inside you and you go back to making coffee and answering emails. The week you had was real. So is the normal now.

The thing nobody tells you is that it'll come back in weird small doorways. A song. The smell of his aftershave on someone in line at the store. You'll find one of his pens in a drawer in two years and lose ten minutes. That's not you being broken; that's just how love that long doesn't leave on a schedule.

A month is also still really early. Your brain has done the first hard part and is letting you breathe. Let it.
