Honestly? I'd wait.

Not because the impulse is bad — wanting to clear the air with someone you used to love is one of the most human things there is. But the combination of "tonight," "after a few drinks," and "long voice memo" is the part I'd push back on. That specific cocktail almost never produces the version of yourself you'd want them to hear. It produces the version that's loose and brave and a little raw, and tomorrow you read the transcript in your head and your stomach drops.

If what you want is to feel good, a voice memo to your ex at 1am is going to give you maybe twenty minutes of feeling good and then a longer stretch of refreshing your phone.

If what you actually want is for *them* to hear something — write it sober first. Tomorrow. Then sit on it a day. If it still feels true after coffee and daylight, send it. The thing worth saying will survive the wait. The thing that wouldn't survive the wait is the thing you're better off not sending.
