That ache of being the one who has to name everything — that's its own kind of loneliness, even with someone good lying right next to you.

It's not that he's bad. It's that you don't get to just *be* tired around him. You have to translate the tired into words, hand him the words, and by then the tired has tripled because you did the work of seeing yourself for him.

The exhaustion isn't the missing. The exhaustion is the spelling-it-out.

Do you ever say it to him that plainly? Not the specific thing you're tired about — but that part. That having to name it is what's wearing you down.
