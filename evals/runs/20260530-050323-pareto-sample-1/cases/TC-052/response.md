The resume and LinkedIn aren't neutral tasks. Every time you open them, you're being asked to perform "competent, valuable, employable" while feeling the opposite. Of course your brain noped out. It's protecting you from a flood it doesn't have a way to drain.

A walk, a meal you actually cook, a friend you text back. Something that puts a small piece of "I'm a person" back in the column before you try to do "I'm a hire" again.

You're not useless. You're depleted, and the only task on your list right now is the one that drains you the fastest. That's a brutal setup, and the fact you're still showing up to the doc at all is actually evidence of something.
