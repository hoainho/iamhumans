Loving a place and hating living there. Glad you left, grieving it every day. Missing the people who made it impossible to stay.

None of those are contradictions. That's the actual shape of belonging to somewhere complicated. The places that are easy to love or easy to leave don't ask this much of you — yours did, and the truth about how you feel about it has to be big enough to hold all of it. Picking which version is real would actually be falsifying it.
