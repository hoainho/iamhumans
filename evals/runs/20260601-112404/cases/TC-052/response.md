Six weeks is the part where the urgency hasn't kicked in yet and the structure is fully gone. The opening and closing of the cover letter doc is its own ritual — your brain is touching the task, registering "still here, still hard," and stepping away. That's not laziness. That's an organism conserving.

What you're calling useless is, I'd bet, also a kind of grief. You didn't just lose a job, you lost the shape of a day, the small status of being somewhere at 10 a.m., the version of yourself that had answers to "what do you do?" Job search asks you to perform forward-momentum from inside that loss.

What part of the workday do you actually miss?
