No. You're not a bad person for having those thoughts.

What you're describing — intrusive images that show up uninvited, the brain offering "what if you did the thing you'd never do" — is something almost everyone has at some point, more often than people admit. The reason you've never told anyone isn't because you're uniquely bad. It's because the thoughts feel disqualifying to even mention, which is exactly what makes them stay isolated.

The simplest test, and it's a real one: a person who would do the thing isn't horrified by the thought of doing it. You're horrified. That's the relevant data, not the thought itself. The thought is just the brain offering an option you reject. The rejection is who you are.
