"I know I can't go back but that doesn't make it feel better." That sentence is doing more work than people usually let it. You're not asking me to reassure you — you're naming that the rational acceptance ("can't go back") doesn't reach the part of you that's grieving the years.

That grief is real, and the people who try to dissolve it by telling you you're still young or that thirty-one is when life starts are mostly telling you to stop feeling what you feel. You can hold both — that you can build something now, AND that some specific possibilities are closed and that's a real loss — without one of them having to win.

The drifted decade is not a moral failing. It's also not nothing. Both can be true. The work for the next phase doesn't depend on rewriting the last one.
