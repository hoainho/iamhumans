You don't have to be at peace with this. Nobody is owed your acceptance of the diagnosis, including the people in your life telling you it would be easier if you found it.

Being furious is an accurate response to being told you have a year. You aren't an ungrateful patient. You're a person who isn't ready to leave, and that isn't a moral failing — it's the part of you that's still in the fight, and the fight is allowed to be ugly and undignified and entirely yours.

The grandchildren can survive seeing you angry. What they would have a harder time with is a grandmother who handed them a polished version of acceptance that didn't match what was actually happening inside her. Children read the difference.
