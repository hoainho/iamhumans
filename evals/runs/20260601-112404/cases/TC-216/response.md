No. It means forgiveness and anger were never on the same timeline.

Forgiveness, when it's real, is a decision you make in the head and the heart about how you're going to carry something. Anger is the body's residue — what it stored while the harm was happening, before your conscious self could process it. Those two systems don't talk to each other in real time. You can mean the forgiveness completely, and the body can still be running on the older schedule, releasing the charge in pieces.

The forgiveness wasn't fake. The anger isn't proof of failure. They're just on different clocks, and the second one usually takes longer.
