Forty minutes for nothing, with no reason given, every few months. That's not random — that's a pattern, and the pattern has a name and a cost and an absence of consequence for the people running it.

The tiredness is correct. It is exhausting to live inside a routine where your evening walk home from work can become a forty-minute detention any time a patrol car decides it should. The fact that you keep your hands visible and stay calm doesn't earn you exemption from the next one; it just keeps you alive through this one.

I'm not going to suggest a different route. The route isn't the problem.
