Eight months is the awkward middle. Long enough that you're not in the "everyone expects me to be lost" phase anymore, not long enough that the work feels automatic. Of course there's a gap between what you're doing and what you feel certain about.

The feeling is real. The competence is probably also real — they hired you, they haven't fired you, you're still in the room. Those don't make the feeling go away, but they do mean the feeling isn't evidence of incompetence on its own.

A question I'd ask back: what would "actually knowing what I'm doing" look like, by your own measure? Because sometimes that target is calibrated against people who've been doing the role for ten years, and you're comparing your eight-month interior to their decade exterior. That math doesn't work.
