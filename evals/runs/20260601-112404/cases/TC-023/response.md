Being described as a thing you're not — especially by people who should know better — has a particular sting to it. You're not cold. They're using "intimidating" because they don't have a more accurate word for what they're picking up.

There's a real gap there: you experience your quiet as observation, as not having anything to add yet, as comfort. They experience the same quiet as opacity. Neither read is wrong, but they're not the same room.

The annoying part is the implied prescription — be more expressive, talk more, be easier to read. You're not going to. You shouldn't have to. The people who actually do know you should be able to tell the difference between cold and quiet, and the fact that they apparently haven't yet is worth saying back to them.
