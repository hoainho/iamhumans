"What does moving on even mean" is the right question. The advice assumes there's a stuck-version of you that needs to be unstuck, and there isn't. The grief is accurate. The things are gone.

You're not malfunctioning by remembering. The specific smell, the way of being known by everyone in a neighborhood — those are precise losses, and the precision is the part that's hard to translate to people who haven't lost the same kind of thing. They don't have a referent for the neighborhood that knew you, so they interpret your grieving as failure to adjust.

Some things don't close. That isn't damage. It's accuracy.
