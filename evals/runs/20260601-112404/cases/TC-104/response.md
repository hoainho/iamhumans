Throw-up nervous is the body taking the meeting seriously before you've had a chance to.

45 minutes is enough time to do exactly one thing: walk somewhere quiet for five minutes, breathe out longer than you breathe in, drink a glass of water. That's it. Don't try to rehearse, don't try to calm down — those are bigger asks than your nervous system can pull off right now.

You can be physically activated and still deliver. They're not the same problem.
