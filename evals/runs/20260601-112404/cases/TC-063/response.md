It makes sense. Lonely-while-around-people is a real, common pattern — not a paradox.

Spending time with people gives you interaction. Loneliness is about something different — being known, or feeling like someone in the room actually has a read on what's going on with you. You can have a calendar full of the first and almost none of the second, and the loneliness keeps showing up because the thing it's about hasn't been touched.

When was the last time someone really got something about you — not the big stuff, just any small specific thing — without you having to translate it for them first?
