The "supposed to care" is the part doing the damage, more than the news itself.

You weren't built to care about every event across eight billion people at the same depth. Nobody is. The human-scale of attention is closer to a few dozen people and a few hundred concerns; the news-scale is closer to infinite. Trying to match the second with the resources for the first is what's burning you out.

Refusing the constant intake isn't moral failure. It's not the same as not caring. You can still care, and even act, on a specific thing — and let the rest of the firehose go past you without each item getting a pound of your attention.
