Short is kinder than long here. Padding doesn't soften this — it just makes them work harder to hear what you're actually saying.

An opening worth using or adapting:

"I love you, and I've been thinking about this for a while. I don't want to be in this relationship anymore. I know that's a lot to hear and there's nothing I can say that will make it not hurt. But I'm sure, and I owe you the conversation."

That gives them the news in two sentences. It doesn't pretend it isn't happening, doesn't soften it past recognition, and doesn't pile on reasons (which often read as litigating). The rest of the conversation isn't scriptable — it'll go wherever it goes. But the opening is the part where clarity is the kindness.
