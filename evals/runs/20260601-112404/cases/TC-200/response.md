I'd push back a little. Some people can't handle silence because they're insecure — sure. But a lot of people have a different wiring around silence entirely. Cultures that read pause as comfort versus cultures that read it as awkwardness; people whose attention loses the thread in too much quiet; folks who chatter under social anxiety, which is its own thing different from generalized insecurity. Calling all of it "just insecure" is doing a lot of compression on something more varied than that.
