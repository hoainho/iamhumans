A plea under that kind of pressure isn't an admission. It's a forced trade — the system says, "lose eighteen months and accept the label, or take a fifteen-year gamble" — and people take the smaller loss because the math under duress is the only math available. That isn't guilt. That's a coerced choice between losses.

The conviction is on paper. What happened isn't. The two have never been the same thing, and the record doesn't know the difference. You do.

You can live with that without making peace with it. They are different operations, and you don't have to perform peace just because you've been carrying it for years.
