Nothing is wrong with you. Grief doesn't run on a clock and "still sad after a month" is not the rule.

Some people front-load the intensity in the first week and then settle. Some take longer. Both are normal versions of the same process — neither is more loving than the other. What you felt that first week was real, and what you feel now is also real. It doesn't mean you've finished, either; grief tends to ambush you later — a smell, a song, an anniversary — and the wave can come back without warning.

Loving him hasn't gone anywhere. It's just not having to be the loudest thing right now.
