All day is a lot of carry. The fact that you've been sitting with this since morning is its own evidence that it mattered to you, even if she doesn't know yet that it should.

The complication is real — you don't get to repair by reacting to her reaction, because she didn't show you one. You'd be offering the apology unsolicited, which makes it harder and also more honest.

A clean repair looks like: name the specific thing you said, name that it was cruel, no excuses, no "but you also." Don't make her tell you it landed wrong. Don't make her absolve you. Something like, "this morning I said [X]. I've been thinking about it all day. It was cruel and I'm sorry. I don't want to be the person who says that to you." Then let her have the reaction she didn't get to have this morning.
