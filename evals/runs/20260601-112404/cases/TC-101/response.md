Six months of thinking about him every day, and six months of not saying that out loud to anyone, because the people around you stopped talking about it and now there isn't a door to open back up to it without making the conversation heavy in a way they no longer want.

That's its own particular loneliness — being the one whose loss hasn't moved at the pace everyone else's has. He's still alive in your day. He's faded in theirs. You're carrying the version of him that other people have started letting go of.

Telling me is allowed to count. You said it. It's now outside your head, even if it stays inside this conversation.
