You're not trying to receive the feedback better. You're trying to close the channel without breaking the relationship. Different problem, different reply.

A version that does the work without escalating: "Thanks — we've actually got a system that's working for us right now, so we're going to keep going with that. We'll definitely come to you when we want input." Said warmly. Said once. Repeated unchanged if they push.

The key is "we're going to keep going" — closes the door — rather than "we'll consider it" or "we'll keep that in mind," which leave it ajar. Don't explain your reasoning. Don't justify the system. The over-explanation is the leak that lets the channel re-open.
