Keep it short and true. The honesty is the kindness here — long explanations invite negotiation; short ones close the door cleanly.

Something like: "I'm so sorry, but I'm not going to be able to make the trip — it's just outside what I can afford right now. I'll be celebrating you from here, and I'd love to take you out when you're back if you're up for it."

That names the real reason, doesn't apologize for your finances, and offers a small alternative that signals you still want to be in her life. Don't elaborate further unless she asks. If she pushes, you can repeat the same one sentence — that's the whole answer, you don't owe a breakdown.
