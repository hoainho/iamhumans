A day in is too soon to know what you feel. The numbness isn't denial — it's the brain not having loaded the file yet. Bodies don't process news this big at the speed news arrives.

The crying might show up tomorrow, or in two weeks, or in pieces over months. There isn't a schedule, and there isn't a wrong way to react in the first 48 hours.

You don't have to tell anyone today. You don't have to tell anyone this week. The information is yours first, and the timing of when other people learn it can be yours to set.
