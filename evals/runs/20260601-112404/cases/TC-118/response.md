"I miss who we used to be" is the more accurate version of the loss than "we don't talk anymore." You aren't just missing the texts. You're missing the version of yourselves that talked every day — the inside jokes, the way the conversation didn't need a reason to start, the years when you were each other's daily weather report.

That version of the friendship isn't necessarily gone for good, but it's also not the one that's currently here. The current friendship is in a different season — three weeks of silence isn't a fight, it's a settling. Sometimes it settles into something quieter that lasts. Sometimes it drifts further. You don't know yet which one this is.

You don't have to fix it tonight, and you also don't have to declare it over. Letting it be unclear for a while is allowed.
