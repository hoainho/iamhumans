Nothing is wrong with you.

Six months in, daily crying for a father is not a malfunction in your grief. It's the size of your father. The "should be further along by now" is a script the world hands you because the world is more comfortable when grief moves on a visible schedule. Your grief doesn't owe the world a tidy curve.

If everyone says it gets easier and it doesn't feel like it's getting easier, the right thing to trust is your daily experience. Not the script.
