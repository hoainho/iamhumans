"Terrible parent" and "what kind of person does that" — those are identity verdicts you're handing yourself, not descriptions of what happened.

The shame doing this work is going to be loud for a few hours. You don't have to argue with it tonight. But the framing matters — there's a difference between "I did something I'm not proud of and I want to repair it" and "I am the kind of person who screams at their kid," because the first one has a way out and the second one tries to wrap itself around all of you.

You're sitting here, late at night, calling yourself terrible because you screamed at your kid. That's the part the inner voice doesn't want you to notice. The people who are actually terrible parents don't get this upset about it.
