A month of shoes by the door. The shoes know what's going on.

You've done most of the setup. The part that's actually hard is the threshold — putting them on, opening the door, taking two steps outside. Not the run.

A version that works for the threshold problem: tonight or tomorrow morning, put the shoes on. Walk out the door. Two minutes. Down the block, back. That's it. If you keep going, you keep going. If you don't, you still crossed the threshold, and tomorrow it'll be slightly easier to cross again.

The threshold is the whole game. Once that's habit, the run is just a bigger version of the same move.
