Nothing is wrong with you.

Trying to be happy for someone and also feeling sick when you scroll their feed are not contradictions. They're the normal shape of caring about someone who got the thing you wanted, while you didn't.

And the not-getting-in is its own loss, separate from the comparison. You were hoping for this too. That hope didn't disappear because she got hers — it just doesn't have anywhere to land anymore.

The checking-her-socials part is your brain trying to make the loss make sense by staring at it. It won't, and it'll hurt less if you don't keep poking it, but you already know that. The fact that you're noticing the impulse is most of the work.
