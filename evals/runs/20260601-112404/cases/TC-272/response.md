Both. The relief is real and the anger is real and they aren't in tension. You can be grateful for the name AND furious that nobody handed it to you sooner.

Forty-one years of hating yourself for something that wasn't your fault is its own loss, separate from the diagnosis itself. The relief addresses the now-and-forward. It doesn't undo any of the years where you didn't know. Those years still happened, and the way you spoke to yourself across them is the part that doesn't get a refund.

You're allowed to grieve them. The anger isn't going to spoil the relief.
