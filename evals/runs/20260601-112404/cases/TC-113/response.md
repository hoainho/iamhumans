The relief is because someone finally named the shape of the thing. Forty-two years of running into the same wall and having no idea why is exhausting in a way that the people around you never quite see, because to them it just looks like inconsistency. Now there's a name, and the name explains a pattern you've been living inside.

The grief is for the version of those forty-two years where you didn't know, and so you took the wall personally. All the times you called yourself lazy, or unreliable, or scattered — and meant it about your character. You can't get those back. Knowing now doesn't undo not-knowing then. That's the part worth crying about, and it's allowed to take a while.
