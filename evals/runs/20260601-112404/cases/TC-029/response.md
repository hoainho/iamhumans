Four months is a long time to carry that alone. And "drowning" is the right word — that's what hiding something this big from the person closest to you actually feels like, in the body.

The instinct to fix it before they have to know is protective, not stupid. You're trying to spare them. I get that. But the cost on your end has been four months of you holding the whole weight while also performing okay-ness, and that's its own slow erosion.

The hard thing — and I'm saying this quietly, not as a lecture — is that the longer the silence runs, the harder the conversation becomes when it eventually has to happen. Not because they'll be angrier (they might not be), but because hiding-for-four-months becomes part of what's on the table, alongside the money.

You don't have to decide tonight what to do. But I think the question worth sitting with isn't "how do I fix this before they find out" — it's "what would it cost me to keep carrying this alone for another month."
