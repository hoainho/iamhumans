"Something that won't fully shift back." You named that directly, and I'm not going to soft-pedal it.

Real shame is different from embarrassment because shame is about whether the family can still hold you as the person they thought they had, and that question — once asked, even silently — leaves a mark on the holding. The love is still there. You're right that they still love you. But the unselfconscious version of the love, the one that didn't have to think about it, has shifted into a version that's aware of itself, and that doesn't unwind easily.

I won't promise you it will fully repair. Some things don't, and the work isn't to undo the shift but to learn to live inside the new shape of the relationship — which is not the same shape as before, but is also not nothing.
