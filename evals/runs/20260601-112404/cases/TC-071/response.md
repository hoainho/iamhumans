If I just said "stop," you'd ignore me, because the part of you doing the calling-yourself-pathetic isn't listening to me — it's listening to itself.

The thing that actually quiets that voice is interrupting it with a different voice. So: if a friend you trusted came to you and said exactly what you just said to yourself, in those exact words — would you call them pathetic for not having figured it out yet? What would you actually say to them?

Whatever you'd say to them is closer to the truth than the line you keep repeating to yourself.
