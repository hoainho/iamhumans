I'm not going to push you toward staying or leaving. That work is yours, and it tends to take longer than the doubt-stage suggests. But one thing worth holding while it unfolds: in most serious traditions, doubt is treated as part of faith, not its opposite. The people who never doubted were usually the people who weren't actually looking. What you're doing now is paying attention. That's not a betrayal of where you came from.

You don't have to resolve this fast. You're allowed to live inside the question for a long time before you have to answer it.
