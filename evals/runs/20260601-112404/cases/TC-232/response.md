That shutdown isn't a character flaw. It's a survival adaptation, and it's a precise one.

A nervous system that goes still and quiet when a man raises his voice learned that response somewhere — at some point in your life, going still kept you safer than reacting. The freezing was load-bearing. The system kept the file, and it's still running the protocol when the cues match — loud man, edge in the voice — even though the current loud man is your boss in a meeting and not whoever it was originally.

The hating-yourself-for-it is the second injury. The system did the job it was built to do; you've just outgrown the situation it was designed for, but it didn't get the memo. That's information about something earlier, not a verdict on who you are now.
