You aren't failing at grief. You're being grief-policed.

"He wouldn't want you to still be grieving this much" is one of those sentences that sounds like it's about him but is actually about the speaker's discomfort with your ongoing sadness. Your sister has reached the part where she'd like the topic to be over, and the form that wish is taking is to invoke your father's preferences as a way to ask you to be done. He doesn't get a vote in that conversation; he's not in the room.

The eight months are yours. The timeline is yours. The shape of your grief is not a performance you're owe-ing them better attendance on.
