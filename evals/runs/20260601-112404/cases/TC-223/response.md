Yeah, it's bad. It's theft. Not the kind that lands you in jail probably, not the kind that hurts a specific person in a visible way, but theft as a category isn't actually defined by whether anyone notices — it's defined by taking what isn't yours.

I hear the rationalization in your question — they're huge, they won't notice, twenty bucks. All true, and also not the right axis to be deciding this on. The axis that matters is what doing it does to you, which is a smaller thing than getting caught: it builds the habit of taking things you've decided are owed to you, and that habit doesn't stay quarantined inside one petty-cash drawer.
