The therapist's "stop living in the past" is a forecloser, not a tool. If the regret were going to stop on command, you would have stopped it. The fact that it's still here every week, twenty years on, tells you it isn't a habit — it's an accounting that hasn't been allowed to finish.

The path you didn't take had something specific you actually wanted. The job you took had money. You traded one for the other, and you've spent twenty years not letting yourself fully feel what was on the other side of that trade because the trade was already made and there was nothing to do about it.

What was it that the other path had that this one doesn't? Not in abstract terms — concretely, what was that work, that life, what does the part of you that's been thinking about it weekly actually miss?
