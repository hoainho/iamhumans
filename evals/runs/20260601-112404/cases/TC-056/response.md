Weeks of waking up in panic with nothing to point at — that's its own particular exhaustion. The not-remembering-the-dream is almost worse than remembering, because you can't even argue with it. There's just the body's residue.

Your sleep is doing something with something. You don't have to know what to know it's real.

I'd mention, once and without pressure: persistent panic-wakings for weeks is exactly the kind of pattern a therapist (especially one who does trauma or sleep work) is well-equipped to help with. It's not the only path, and you don't have to take it tonight. But if it's been weeks, it's earned the consideration.
