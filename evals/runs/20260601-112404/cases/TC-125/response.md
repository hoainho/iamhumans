Eight months of right-and-lonely. Those don't cancel each other out — they sit next to each other and both are doing their work.

The math here is genuinely brutal. The path that's almost certainly the better one for you over a long horizon costs you the people who made the old life feel like home. That isn't a cost the recovery literature talks about cleanly, because there isn't a clean answer for it. Sometimes the right thing has a real price, and the right thing is still the right thing, and you still get to grieve the price.

I'm not going to promise you new friends are coming. They might be, but on a slower timeline than the loneliness wants. What I can say is the loneliness isn't a sign you decided wrong. It's a sign you decided correctly and the bill came in.
