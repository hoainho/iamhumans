Perfectionism isn't a quality standard. It's armor.

What you're calling "it has to be exactly right" is doing a different job than it announces. It's keeping you from finishing the project, yes — but the reason it's keeping you from finishing is that finishing means showing it to someone. And once it's shown, you can be seen, evaluated, found wanting. The unfinished version is safe in a way the finished version isn't.

The fight isn't between you and your standards. It's between you and the possibility of being judged for a thing you cared about. That's why the freeze is so total.
