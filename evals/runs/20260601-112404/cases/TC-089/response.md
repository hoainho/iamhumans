"Should be happy" is doing the work of the disappointment here. You're measuring your real feelings against the version where you were supposed to be lit up about this, and the gap between them is what reads as wrongness.

Promotions almost always come with losses that don't show up in the announcement. More time in management, less time in the thing you were good at and probably loved doing. More politics, more visibility, more performance. The pay and title go up; what you spend your day on changes. That trade is sometimes worth it and sometimes not, and the not-happy is your honest first read of which one this is.

What's actually different about your day now compared to three months ago? Concretely — what are you doing more of, what are you doing less of?
