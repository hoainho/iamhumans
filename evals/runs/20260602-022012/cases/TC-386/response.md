You're describing two systems that learned to handle distress in opposite directions. Yours reaches outward — talking, processing, getting reassurance is how it tries to settle. His pulls inward — going quiet, reducing input is how his settles. Neither one is the wrong move; they just land hard on each other.

From inside yours, his quiet feels like a door closing. From inside his, your reaching feels like the room getting louder when he needs it quieter. Both of those are accurate from where you're each standing. The stuck part is real — it's not because one of you is doing it wrong.
