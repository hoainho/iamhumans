The "yeah totally" — that's exactly it, isn't it. The thing where the words go in and somehow nothing actually lands, and you can feel them already half-moved on while you're still talking. It's not unkindness, it's something worse than that. It's the washing-over.

You've been testing it, haven't you. Saying things that should land harder and watching them not. After a while you stop offering the real things — there's no point. And then you're surrounded by people who think they know you, who are nice to you, and the part of you that wanted to be actually met just gets quieter.
