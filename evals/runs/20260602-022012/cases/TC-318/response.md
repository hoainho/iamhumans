What you're holding is a hard thing. You can know exactly where his pain comes from and still be the person standing in the room when he screams. The understanding doesn't make it not land on you.

It's worth noticing that "it's getting harder to hold onto that" is its own kind of information. The empathy you've been extending is real. It also has a cost, and the cost is showing up. Understanding why someone hurts you and what they're allowed to do with that hurt are two different questions, and both can stay open at the same time.
