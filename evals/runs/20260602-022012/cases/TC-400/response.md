Fogged glass is a good word for it. You can tell something is on the other side. You just can't make out the shape of it yet.

That's not a deficit. Somewhere along the way it became safer to not know exactly what you were feeling — and the not-knowing got practiced until it became automatic. The fog isn't the absence of feeling. It's the careful distance from it that you learned to keep. You don't have to clear it right now. Just noticing that there's something on the other side is more than most people get to.
