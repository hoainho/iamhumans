The work you've done is real. The understanding, the letting go of blame — that took years and it's yours. And it doesn't have to close the ache.

Some of what aches doesn't have a name because it was never there to be named. You can't really grieve a specific moment of being seen if no one ever sat down to see you in the first place — the absence is shaped like a thing that was supposed to happen and didn't. That doesn't mean something's still wrong with you or that there's more therapy to do. It just means understanding why doesn't always reach the place where the thing was missing.
