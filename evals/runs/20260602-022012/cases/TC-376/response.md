Hating yourself for the explosions is its own separate layer on top of the explosions — and it doesn't help. It just adds shame to something that's already hard. The thing nobody tells you is that staying calm isn't really a trait. It's a thing that gets built, slowly, by being around someone who could bring you back to calm when you were small. If that wasn't really there, the muscle never fully grew in. That's not a character flaw. That's a missing piece you were never handed.
