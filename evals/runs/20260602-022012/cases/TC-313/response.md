Three years is a long time to have to go back through and re-read. The vacation, the night he said it — you're not going crazy. The ground actually shifted, and now every memory has to be re-examined to see what was standing on solid ground and what wasn't. That's not paranoia. That's what your mind has to do when the story it was living in turns out to have been a different story.

The feeling of going insane is the feeling of having to rewrite something you thought was finished.
