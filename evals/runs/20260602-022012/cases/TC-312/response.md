That fear makes sense, and the love makes sense, and neither cancels out the other. Closeness and desire don't always live in the same place — sometimes the very thing that makes a relationship feel safe (no gap, no mystery, totally known) is the same thing that quiets the physical pull. That's not either of you being broken. It is genuinely scary, though, because the thing you have is real and you don't want to lose any part of it.
