A container with nothing in it. That image is doing a lot of work — it's not just empty, it's something that was built to hold whatever someone else poured in.

That doesn't usually come from nowhere. Somewhere early on, having a self of your own probably felt less safe than being whatever the person you were with needed. The "empty when alone" isn't proof there's nothing in there. It's that you never got to find out what was, because there was always someone else's shape to fill first.
