The part of you that went quiet at that table is the part that's still around eight. The age where being dismissed by your brother felt like disappearing — that part doesn't know 28 years have gone by. It's still right there, at the dinner table you grew up at.

"Why does this still happen" is such a tired question to be carrying around. The answer isn't that something is wrong with you. Some things just don't age out the way the rest of us does. The dinner is over now. That eight-year-old is still somewhere in there, and she probably still needs something — even if it isn't from him.
