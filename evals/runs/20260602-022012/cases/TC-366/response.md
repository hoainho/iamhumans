I don't think needing people when you're struggling is weakness. I actually think it's what people are for.

The "I should be able to handle it alone" thing — I want to ask where that rule came from, because it doesn't match how humans are built. The ones who can reach for someone when things are hard are usually also the ones who can show up when someone else is hard. The two things aren't opposites. Going to someone when you're hurting isn't a failure of self-sufficiency. It's just the thing.

What's weak about wanting to not be alone with it?
