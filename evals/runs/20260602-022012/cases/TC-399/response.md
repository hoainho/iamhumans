"Something got turned off." That's not a small thing to notice in a mirror at the start of a day.

Responsible, shows up, doesn't cause trouble — those words read like a description someone else might have written about you and that you eventually agreed to be. There's usually a version of a person underneath that one. Quieter, more particular, with actual preferences and things she used to want for no good reason. I don't think she's gone. I think she's been waiting, and that waiting takes a long time to register as the absence you saw this morning.
