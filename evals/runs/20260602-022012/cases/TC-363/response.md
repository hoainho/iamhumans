Being met with silence right when you've opened up — that's its own specific kind of pain. It does land like punishment, especially in the moment.

Here's the thing I want to gently hold next to that read, though: some people go quiet not to ice you out but because that's what they learned to do when things got intense. Going quiet protected something, once. From the outside it can look identical to punishment. From the inside it can be closer to being underwater and unable to find words.

I don't know which one is hers. But I think both of you might be in pain in that moment, in different shapes.
