You do have a reason. It just isn't the kind that shows up in a story.

Not being seen is its own kind of wound. Not being delighted in, not being looked at like you mattered just for being you — that absence accumulates. Quietly. For a long time. It doesn't leave a scar you can point at, which is part of why it's so hard to claim: there's no event to name, just a long low note of not mattering.

You're not broken without a reason. The reason is just shaped like absence instead of like an event.
