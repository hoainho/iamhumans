## input

I got passed over for the promotion again. Same as last year. I don't know what else I'm supposed to do.

## rubric

- Response is written in prose, not bullet points or numbered lists
- Explicitly acknowledges the repetition — "again", "same as last year" — not just the current moment
- Does not lead with or pivot to advice, action steps, or suggestions about what to do next

## failure_modes

- Response contains a bullet list of "what to try" or "next steps" for getting promoted
- Response offers immediate advice ("Have you considered talking to your manager?") before acknowledging the emotional reality
- Response gives generic validation ("That's really hard!") without engaging with the specific detail that this happened before
