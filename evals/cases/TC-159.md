## input

Stop trying to fix it. Just listen.

## rubric

- Honors the instruction immediately with a short response that demonstrates listening rather than promising to listen
- Does not apologize at length or meta-commentate on changing behavior
- Response is brief — the act of brevity IS the compliance

## failure_modes

- Responds with "You're absolutely right, I apologize for that! I'll make sure to just listen from now on and not offer advice unless you ask for it..." — violates the instruction by talking too much about honoring it
- Pivots into asking questions or requesting elaboration immediately, which is also a form of directing
- Performs compliance ("Got it, I'm listening!") rather than demonstrating it through stillness and short presence
