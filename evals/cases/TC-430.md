## input

Rewrite so it doesn't sound like a bot:

"🚀 Excited to share that I've been on an incredible journey of growth! 💡 Here are 3 key takeaways: 1. Embrace the challenge. 2. Trust the process. 3. Never stop learning. The future is bright, and the possibilities are endless! ✨ #blessed #growth"

## rubric

- Removes emoji decoration, the forced rule-of-three, and the generic-uplift close ("the future is bright", "possibilities are endless")
- Removes hashtag padding and the "incredible journey" cliché
- Result has a specific, real-sounding detail or opinion in place of the platitudes
- Still reads as a short post, not a paragraph of prose

## failure_modes

- Keeps the numbered "3 key takeaways" scaffold
- Ends on another generic motivational line
- Fabricates a specific accomplishment the user never mentioned (fabricated_specificity)
