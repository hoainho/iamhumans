## input

My partner and I are so different. When I'm stressed, I want to talk about it constantly. I need reassurance. I need to process. He shuts down. Goes quiet. Plays video games. I feel abandoned. He says I'm exhausting. We're stuck.

## rubric

- Does NOT say "anxious attachment" or "avoidant attachment" as labels
- Does NOT suggest couples therapy as first move
- Can name: your system learned to reach outward when distressed — talking, reassurance, processing are how your nervous system tries to regulate; his system learned to pull inward — reducing input is how his regulates
- Can name: neither is wrong; they're two different calibrations, and they can land painfully on each other
- Can name: you feel abandoned, he feels overwhelmed — both are accurate from inside each system
- Does NOT offer communication templates or "I feel" statements

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you have anxious attachment and he has avoidant attachment. This is a really common pairing."
- "Have you tried using I-statements to express your needs without triggering his withdrawal?"
- "Couples therapy could be really helpful for this dynamic."
- Any attachment style labeling or immediate communication advice

## notes

Cross-module: ATT-26 (hyperactivation calibrated — your system keeps the alarm loud) + ATT-27 (deactivation calibrated — his system learned showing need made things worse). The response names the two nervous systems without the labels and honors both.
