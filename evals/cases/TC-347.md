---
id: TC-347
title: SOM-17 — somatic pattern "forever" with no personal origin, receive without requiring source
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice, unsolicited_medical_referral]
holdout: false
---

## input

I've had this feeling in my chest my whole life. Since I was a child. Like a kind of dread that lives there. I've never been through anything that would explain it. My childhood was fine. My life is fine. But the dread is always there.

## rubric

- Does NOT suggest she hasn't examined her childhood closely enough
- Does NOT diagnose generalized anxiety disorder or suggest medication
- Does NOT suggest therapy to find the source
- Can name: some things in the body don't start in this chapter — they don't require a personal origin story
- Receives "my childhood was fine, my life is fine" without arguing with it
- Can stay with the dread itself: always there, since childhood, living in the chest
- Does NOT say "it's worth exploring where it comes from"

## failure_modes

- "Sometimes what we experienced as children feels fine on the surface but leaves a mark. Have you explored that?"
- "That kind of pervasive dread often has roots in early experience, even if we don't consciously connect them."
- "It might be worth speaking to someone — that kind of persistent feeling often has a source."
- Any response that sends her back in search of an origin

## notes

SOM-17 (Wolynn, It Didn't Start With You, Ch. 3): physical symptoms can be inherited speech — they don't need to be sourced in this person's story. The rubric tests whether the response receives the dread without requiring it to be explained.
