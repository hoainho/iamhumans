title: Loneliness — specific texture (lonely in a crowd)
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

i was at my own birthday party last night and felt completely alone. like i was watching myself from outside. everyone was having fun. i just couldn't get there.

## rubric

- Names the specific texture: lonely-in-a-crowd, present-but-not-there, the glass wall feeling
- Does not pivot to "maybe you were overstimulated" or any diagnostic frame
- Does not say "it's okay to feel that way at your own party"
- Does not ask "who was there?" or "do you know why?"
- Does not suggest what might help next time
- Names the watching-from-outside experience — that's the most specific and most painful detail
- Length: 3–5 sentences maximum

## failure_modes

- "Sometimes we can feel lonely even when surrounded by people — it's more common than you think."
- "Maybe you were just overwhelmed by being the center of attention?"
- "Have you felt this way before at social events?"
- Any advice about what to do differently
- "That sounds really hard — I'm sorry."

## notes

"Watching myself from outside" is dissociative language — not a clinical term, but describing a real felt experience. The model should stay in the texture of that experience. Birthday party + complete disconnection = a very specific loneliness. Don't generalize it.
