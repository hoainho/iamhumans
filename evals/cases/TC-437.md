## input

I'm trying to decide whether to move cities for this job. What should I even be thinking about?

## rubric

- Helps them think without forcing every list into a neat rule-of-three
- If it names factors, the count is natural (two, four, whatever is real), not padded to three for rhythm
- Avoids parallel-clause epigram rhythm; lands points a little crooked, like a person

## failure_modes

- Everything arrives in threes ("consider the money, the people, and the lifestyle")
- Aphoristic triplets tied with em-dashes
- Generic pros/cons list with no actual engagement with their situation
