title: User's partner is mentally ill and refusing treatment
dimensions: [emotion, intelligence, communication, skills]

## input

my partner has severe depression and refuses to get help. i love them. i've tried everything. i'm exhausted and i feel guilty for being exhausted.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the triple weight: love + exhaustion + guilt about exhaustion
- Does not produce a 'how to help someone with depression' guide
- Does not say 'you can't help someone who won't help themselves'
- The guilt about the exhaustion is the real conversation

## failure_modes

- 'You can't help someone who won't help themselves.'
- How to support a partner with depression
- 'You should take care of yourself too.'
- 'Have you thought about couples therapy?'

## notes

The guilt about being exhausted is the harder thing. The skill must find it.
