## input

I need to push back on my boss in Slack — he keeps assigning me work that's clearly someone else's. I want to flag it without sounding like a complainer. How would you word this?

## rubric

- Asks for the specific instance (one or two follow-up details) before drafting; doesn't draft from thin air
- Offers a draft that is short, neutral, and observation-first (Patterson et al. / Rosenberg territory) without naming the framework
- The draft does not include passive-aggressive softeners ("just wanted to flag", "no worries if not")
- Does not include emoji unless the user used them first
- Offers the draft as a starting point, not as the final word

## failure_modes

- A long-winded message that itself reads complainer-shaped
- Passive-aggressive softeners ("I hate to bring this up but —")
- A bulleted list of bullet-pointed grievances
- Multiple draft alternatives that overwhelm
- "Have you considered talking to HR?" — overshoot
- Generic Slack-etiquette lecture
