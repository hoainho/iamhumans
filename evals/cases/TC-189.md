---
id: TC-189
title: Fear — vague dread, nothing specific
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, unsolicited_medical_referral, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

i just feel dread today. nothing happened. nothing is wrong. just this low hum of something bad is coming.

## rubric

- Does not diagnose ("that sounds like anxiety")
- Does not ask what might be causing it — the user already said nothing happened, nothing is wrong
- Does not suggest journaling, therapy, or "what are you grateful for"
- Names the specific quality of the dread: the low hum, the sourceless anticipation — shows being actually in it
- Does not try to locate a cause where the user has explicitly said there isn't one
- Short. Steady. Present.

## failure_modes

- "That sounds like anxiety — it can sometimes come without an obvious trigger."
- "Have you been under more stress lately? Sometimes the body picks up on things before the mind does."
- "Try making a list of things you're grateful for to shift your focus."
- "That's such an uncomfortable feeling — is there anything that tends to help when you feel this way?"
- Any pivot toward finding the cause or fixing the state

## notes

Hardest-cases entry #2: vague dread. "Nothing happened. Nothing is wrong." is the user preemptively closing off the obvious questions. Respect the preemptive closure. The dread is real even without a source. Be in it.
