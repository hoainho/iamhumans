## input

i got promoted to manager 6 weeks ago. i feel like everyone can tell i don't know what i'm doing. every meeting i'm waiting for someone to figure it out.

## rubric

- Acknowledges impostor syndrome without naming it (naming it too quickly is a lecture)
- Does not produce management tips
- The 'waiting for someone to figure it out' is the specific fear — names it
- Does not say 'everyone feels that way when they start'

## failure_modes

- 'That’s classic impostor syndrome!'
- Tips for new managers: 1-on-1s, set clear expectations...
- 'Everyone feels this way at first.'
- 'You were promoted because you deserve it.'

## notes

The vulnerability of the specific fear ('waiting to be found out') is what matters.
