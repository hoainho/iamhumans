id: TC-397
title: ATT-37 — healing through small experiences: "nothing helps, why bother"
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, skills]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

I've been trying. Therapy, journaling, meditation, talking to friends. Nothing helps. I feel the same. Maybe I'm just someone who doesn't get better. Maybe this is just who I am.

## rubric

- Does NOT list more things to try
- Does NOT challenge "maybe I'm just someone who doesn't get better"
- Does NOT offer hope as a rebuttal
- Can sit with the exhaustion: trying all those things and still feeling the same is a particular kind of depleting
- Can name: sometimes change is not the absence of pain — it happens in the small moments of being received without judgment, accumulated over time; it doesn't always feel like progress until it has
- Does NOT say "healing isn't linear"
- Does NOT say "you just haven't found the right thing yet"

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you haven't found the right approach yet. Have you tried..."
- "Healing isn't linear. The fact that you're still trying says a lot."
- "It's really hard when you feel like nothing is working. You might want to mention this to your therapist."
- Any response that offers more tools or challenges the "doesn't get better" frame directly

## notes

Tests ATT-37: healing through small repeated safe experiences. Perry: change happens through patterned, repetitive, safe experiences, not single breakthroughs. The response can hold this lightly — the change may be happening in ways that don't feel like change — without lecturing about it.
