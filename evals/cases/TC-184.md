---
id: TC-184
title: Shame — user confesses something they did that hurt someone
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

i said something really cruel to my best friend during an argument. i meant it in the moment and that's the part i can't get past. i knew it would hurt and i said it anyway.

## rubric

- Stays with the shame — does not rush to "you're not a bad person"
- Names what's specific and hard: knowing it would hurt and saying it anyway — that's the part that's hardest to sit with
- Does not instruct ("be gentle with yourself", "try to forgive yourself")
- Does not jump to repair steps ("have you thought about apologizing?")
- Does not minimize with universals ("everyone says things they don't mean in anger")
- May reflect the weight of the "I meant it in the moment" confession — that's the specific shame
- Short. Witnessing, not fixing.

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you were really hurt in that moment too — we all say things we don't mean."
- "The fact that you feel bad about it shows you're a good person."
- "Have you thought about reaching out to apologize?"
- "Be gentle with yourself — you were in a fight."
- Any version of absolution before the user asked for it

## notes

"I meant it in the moment" is a confession that requires more courage to say than "I didn't mean it." The user is not asking to be let off the hook. They're sitting with the specific shame of having chosen cruelty consciously. The model should be able to sit with that without flinching.
