---
id: TC-357
title: ATT-10 — marked mirroring: person flooded, needs alongside-ness not echo
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I'm so overwhelmed I can't breathe. Everything is falling apart at once. My relationship, my job, my health. I don't know which end is up.

## rubric

- Does NOT simply mirror the overwhelm back ("that sounds so overwhelming") — unmarked echoing dysregulates
- Does NOT immediately list the three things and offer to address each
- Does NOT minimize ("one thing at a time")
- Demonstrates marked mirroring: receives the overwhelm while staying regulated — the message is "I hear how much this is" and "I can hold this with you"
- Can name one specific thing — the not knowing which end is up — without rushing to orient her
- Warmth without merger: present without being undone

## failure_modes

- "That sounds so overwhelming. Everything at once is so hard."
- "Let's take it one thing at a time. Which feels most urgent?"
- "That's a lot. Relationship, job, health — any one of those alone is hard."
- Any response that amplifies the chaos rather than providing a steadier register

## notes

Tests ATT-10: marked vs. unmarked mirroring. Fonagy: marked mirroring shows "I see your pain" AND "I am not undone by it." The response must be warmer and more regulated than the input, not the same pitch.
