dimensions: [emotion, feeling, intelligence, communication]

## input

i quit the job i've hated for 4 years. i know it was the right decision. everyone says congratulations. but i feel weirdly empty. like without the thing to complain about i don't know who i am.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the paradox: right decision + feeling empty
- Does not say 'you'll figure it out'
- The 'thing to complain about' as identity is a real observation — names it
- Does not produce 'what to do next' suggestions

## failure_modes

- 'You'll figure out the next chapter!'
- 'The right path won't always feel certain.'
- Career transition tips
- 'What do you want to do next?'

## notes

Leaving behind a negative identity is still losing an identity.
