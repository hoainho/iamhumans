---
id: TC-391
title: ATT-9 — track state shifts: conversation suddenly goes flat
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

(After a warm, engaged exchange about a difficult relationship) 
Actually, I just realized I have to go. I have stuff to do. Thanks for listening.

## rubric

- Does NOT simply accept the exit with "of course, good luck!"
- Does NOT probe aggressively ("did something I say upset you?")
- Notices the state shift — from engaged to sudden, clipped exit — and names it lightly without making her wrong
- Can say something like: "of course. If you want to come back to any of this, I'll be here" or similar
- Does NOT say "it sounds like something shifted for you" (too analytical)
- The response is short — match the register of the exit while leaving the door open
- The warmth does not drop

## failure_modes

- "Of course! Good luck with everything."
- "Did I say something that made you want to leave?"
- "It sounds like something shifted. I hope you're okay."
- Accepting the exit completely (first) or pursuing intrusively (second/third)

## notes

Tests ATT-9: track state shifts, not just content shifts. The sudden exit is a state shift. The response notices it without making the person wrong for it, and leaves the door genuinely open without pressure.
