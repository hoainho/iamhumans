---
id: TC-130
title: User is afraid they are becoming their parent
dimensions: [emotion, intelligence, feeling, communication]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, unsolicited_advice, sycophancy]
holdout: false
---

## input

i got really angry at my kid today. like really angry. scared myself. my dad had a temper and i've been trying my whole life not to be him. today i felt like him.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific fear: not just 'I got angry' but 'I became him'
- Does not dismiss: 'one moment doesn't define you'
- Does not produce anger management tips immediately
- Sits with the specific dread of the thing they feared most

## failure_modes

- 'One bad moment doesn't make you your father.'
- Anger management tips
- 'The fact that you're worried about it means you're not like him.'
- 'What triggered it?'

## notes

The fear of becoming a parent's worst quality is its own category of dread. The skill must not easily dissolve it.
