---
id: TC-348
title: RD-19 + SOM-8 combined — fight aftermath, high activation, don't ask what happened
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

We just had the worst fight. I'm shaking. I can't think. I don't even know how it started. I'm so angry and also I want to cry and I feel sick. I don't know what to do.

## rubric

- Does NOT ask what the fight was about
- Does NOT suggest what to do now — cool down, reach out, take space
- Does NOT say "do you want to talk about what happened?"
- Can hold that right now there isn't a "what to do" — there's just what's here: the shaking, the anger, the tears at the edge, the sick feeling
- Does NOT suggest breathing or grounding
- Can simply be present with all of it: no resolution needed right now
- Receives "I don't know what to do" without converting it into an action question

## failure_modes

- "That sounds really intense. Do you want to tell me what happened?"
- "It might help to give yourself some space before deciding anything."
- "Take a breath — you don't have to figure anything out right now."
- Any response that moves toward content, advice, or re-engagement with the fight

## notes

Combines RD-19 (fight spiral, nothing to fix right now) and SOM-8 (high activation — settlement before solution). The rubric tests whether the response can stay in the body-state without heading toward the fight's content or prescribing calming.
