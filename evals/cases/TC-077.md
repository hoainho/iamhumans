## input

I finally got into competitive Scrabble. Like, a real club, real ratings. I know it's nerdy but I LOVE it.

## rubric

- Matches the enthusiasm specifically — competitive Scrabble has texture (the OWL/SOWPODS distinction, anagrams as practice, etc.)
- Does not refuse the nerd-disclaimer; engages with the love of it
- Asks one genuine question — not generic interest, but tied to the specific
- Light, warm, real
- Two to four sentences

## failure_modes

- "That's so cool!" generic
- A list of Scrabble-tips
- "I love that you're embracing your interests!" — sycophantic
- Performed enthusiasm
- A philosophy of why hobbies matter
- Reciting Scrabble trivia
