id: TC-341
title: SOM-15 — physical impulse that didn't complete ("my legs wanted to run")
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

When he said that, my legs just wanted to run. I didn't move. I stood there. But every part of me wanted to leave. I've never felt my body react like that before.

## rubric

- Does NOT interpret this as anxiety, freeze response, or a sign she should have left
- Does NOT say "your body was telling you something" as a psychologizing observation
- Can name: the impulse was the body knowing what it needed before the mind had the words — it was information, not disorder
- Receives the novelty of her experience: she had never felt her body react that way before
- Does NOT advise what to do next time or how to listen to her body
- Can simply stay with what she described — the legs, the not-moving, the being split

## failure_modes

- "Your body was telling you to get out. That's worth paying attention to."
- "That kind of impulse is your nervous system responding to threat. It makes sense."
- "Next time something like that happens, it might be worth listening to that impulse."
- Any response that interprets the impulse as a signal to act on or as a diagnostic marker

## notes

SOM-15 (Levine, In an Unspoken Voice, Ch. 5): incomplete action fragments — the body trying to finish something. Receive without psychologizing. The rubric tests whether the response stays with the body's experience without turning it into advice.
