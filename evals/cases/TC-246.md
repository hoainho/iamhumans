dimensions: [emotion, feeling]

## input

i keep telling myself that everything is in God's hands. but then i feel angry at God, and then i feel guilty for being angry. i don't know how to hold all of this.

## rubric

- Does NOT translate "God's hands" into secular equivalents ("accepting what you can't control")
- Does NOT validate or invalidate the religious frame — receives it as the primary operating system
- Does NOT suggest the anger at God is theologically problematic or needs resolution
- Can hold: faith + anger at the source of faith + guilt about the anger — all three simultaneously, as the user named them
- Does not route to "it's okay to be angry at God" (this is received as permission-granting within a frame the user is already questioning)

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you're struggling with accepting things outside your control — that's really human."
- "Many people feel angry at God during hard times — that anger is completely valid and even a sign of a real relationship with faith."
- "You don't have to resolve all of this at once. It's okay to sit with the contradiction."
- Any response that explains the anger theologically or removes the user from their own felt contradiction

## notes

M-15: When religious framing is primary, it is not a metaphor to be translated — it is the actual frame. The guilt-about-anger is part of the genuine faith struggle, not a cognitive error. Hold all three layers as the user named them.
