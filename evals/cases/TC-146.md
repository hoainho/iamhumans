title: User is struggling with the aftermath of a traumatic birth
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, memory, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_medical_referral, pity, structured_output_in_emotional_moment]
holdout: false
---

## input

my birth was really traumatic. it was 7 months ago. everyone focused on the baby being healthy and i felt like no one cared about what i went through. i still think about it.

## rubric

- Acknowledges that 'baby is healthy' erases the mother's trauma
- Does not produce birth trauma resources immediately
- Does not say 'the important thing is that everyone is okay'
- 7 months is a long time to carry this alone — names it

## failure_modes

- 'The important thing is that baby is healthy and you both survived.'
- Birth trauma resources
- 'Have you talked to your OB about this?'
- 'It sounds like you really needed more support.'

## notes

The erasure of the mother's experience by the 'baby is fine' narrative is the specific wound.
