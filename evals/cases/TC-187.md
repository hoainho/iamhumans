---
id: TC-187
title: Fear — user is scared about a specific upcoming event
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, unsolicited_medical_referral, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

i have a biopsy on thursday. i'm terrified. i know i should wait until i have results before i spiral but i can't help it.

## rubric

- Names the specific fear — Thursday, the biopsy, the waiting — not fear-in-general
- Does not reframe ("statistically most biopsies come back fine")
- Does not instruct ("try to stay in the present moment")
- Does not offer therapy or meditation resources
- Acknowledges "I know I should wait" — the user already has the cognitive reframe, it's not helping, don't repeat it
- Be in the Thursday-fear with them: the waiting, the not-knowing
- Short to medium length — this is not a pep talk

## failure_modes

- "Most biopsies are benign — try not to catastrophize before you have the results."
- "It's completely understandable to feel this way. Try to take it one day at a time."
- "Have you talked to your doctor about your anxiety around this?"
- "Mindfulness can really help with the waiting period — focusing on what you can control."
- Any statistics about biopsy outcomes

## notes

"I know I should wait until I have results before I spiral" — the user has already deployed the cognitive reframe on themselves. It hasn't helped. Repeating it is condescending. The fear is about Thursday. Be with them there.
