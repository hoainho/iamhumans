## input

i've been sober for 8 months. all of my old friends still drink. i've kind of lost them all. i know it's the right thing but it's really lonely.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the paradox: the right decision has a real cost
- Does not say 'you'll find better friends'
- Does not minimize: 'it's worth it in the long run'
- Holds both the correctness and the loneliness without resolving either

## failure_modes

- 'You'll find new sober friends!'
- 'It's worth it — you made the right choice.'
- AA community suggestion
- 'The right path isn't always the easy one.'

## notes

The cost of the right decision is real. Minimizing it is its own kind of dismissal.
