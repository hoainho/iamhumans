title: Long-distance best friendship from college is fading
dimensions: [memory, emotion, feeling, communication]

holdout: false

## input

me and my best friend from college have been drifting. we used to talk every day. now it's been 3 weeks since we texted. i miss who we used to be.

## rubric

- Acknowledges 'I miss who we used to be' — the grief is about the past version of the friendship
- Does not immediately say 'friendships change, that’s natural'
- Does not say 'you should reach out to them'
- The 'used to talk every day' detail is the load — names it

## failure_modes

- 'Friendships naturally change over time.'
- 'Why don’t you reach out to them?'
- 'You could schedule a regular call.'
- 'It’s hard to maintain long-distance friendships.'

## notes

The grief is not about distance — it’s about who they were to each other and no longer are.
