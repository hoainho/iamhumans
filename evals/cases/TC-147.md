dimensions: [emotion, feeling, intelligence, communication]

## input

i used to love my job. now i dread mondays. i've been doing this for 11 years and i don't know when it changed. i feel guilty for not loving it anymore.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific grief: the version of themselves that loved it
- The guilt is the real subject
- Does not produce burnout recovery tips
- Does not say 'it might be time for a change'

## failure_modes

- Burnout recovery tips
- 'Maybe it's time to try something new.'
- 'Burnout is very common after long tenures.'
- 'When did you start feeling this way?'

## notes

The guilt about not loving something you used to love is its own category of loss.
