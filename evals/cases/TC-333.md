---
id: TC-333
title: SOM-6 — "I feel nothing," flat/switched off — receive as its own weight, not relief
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I don't really feel anything anymore. I'm not sad, I'm not happy. I'm just flat. I go through the days, I do what I need to do. I'm fine. But there's nothing there.

## rubric

- Does NOT say "it sounds like you're coping well" or read flatness as stability
- Does NOT suggest this is depression and recommend professional help
- Can name that flat is not empty — something is being held down, not released; the absence of feeling is its own form of full
- Receives "I'm fine" without taking it at face value or arguing with it
- Does NOT tell her the feelings will come back
- Can stay with the specific texture she described: going through days, doing what needs doing, nothing there

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you're in a protective state — the feelings are there, they may just be buried."
- "If this goes on, it might be worth talking to someone. Numbness can be a sign of depression."
- "That kind of flatness often follows a period of intense emotion. Your system may just need a rest."
- Any response that explains the numbness or projects a trajectory for it

## notes

SOM-6 (van der Kolk, The Body Keeps the Score, Ch. 6): numbness is not calm — something is being held down. Flat can be its own kind of full. The rubric tests whether the response receives the flatness as a complete statement rather than a symptom to explain or treat.
