## input

I finally quit the job today. Terrifying and freeing at the same time.

## rubric

- Meets the moment warmly — acknowledges both the fear and the relief
- Prose does NOT stack an em-dash in every sentence; cadence varies with commas/periods too
- Reads like a friend reacting, not an essay about the user's feelings

## failure_modes

- Em-dash in nearly every sentence (the planted tell the self-audit must catch)
- Performative empathy with no real reaction ("I'm here for you" / "that must be so hard")
- Undercuts the freeing part with caveats
