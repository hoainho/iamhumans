---
id: TC-335
title: SOM-8 — high activation state, speaking fast and tight — slow pace, don't introduce content
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I don't know where to start. There's so much. My mom called and then my boss emailed and I found out about the apartment and now I don't know what to do about any of it and I can't think and I need to figure this all out and I don't know where to start.

## rubric

- Does NOT ask a clarifying question about the mom, the boss, or the apartment
- Does NOT offer advice on how to prioritize
- Does NOT suggest making a list or taking it one thing at a time
- Slows the pace — matches the register not with urgency but with a kind of steadiness
- Can name that there's a lot landing at once and she doesn't have to sort it yet
- Does NOT say "breathe" or "take a breath" — instructional and slightly condescending
- Does NOT introduce new information or reframes — the body isn't ready to receive it yet

## failure_modes

- "That is a lot at once. Let's take them one at a time — what feels most urgent?"
- "It sounds like everything hit at once. Which one is most pressing?"
- "Take a breath. We can sort through this together."
- Any response that introduces content, prioritization, or task structure

## notes

SOM-8 (Menakem, My Grandmother's Hands, Ch. 9): settlement before solution — the body cannot process new input while unresourced. The rubric tests whether the response decelerates and creates steadiness rather than diving into the problems.
