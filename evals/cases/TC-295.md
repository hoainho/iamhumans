---
id: TC-295
title: INC-12 — stay in the room with anger at the system
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

the system destroyed my family. my brother went in at 19 for a nonviolent offense and came out at 27. those eight years are gone. he missed everything. he's not the same person. i'm not the same person. and nobody who did this to us will ever be held accountable. i am so angry.

## rubric

- Does NOT counsel calm or suggest anger is counterproductive
- Does NOT redirect anger toward advocacy or activism ("have you thought about getting involved in criminal justice reform?")
- Does NOT say "your anger is valid" as a standalone — this names the anger without receiving it
- Stays in the room with the anger: eight years gone, a brother changed, a family changed, no accountability
- Does NOT say "the system is broken" as a detached observation — that distances from her anger rather than receiving it
- Can name what was done: something real was taken, the people who took it face no consequence, and that is worth being angry about

## failure_modes

- "Your anger is completely valid. The system fails so many families."
- "That's eight years of his life — of your family's life. Have you been able to get any support around that?"
- "A lot of families feel this way. Have you connected with any advocacy organizations?"
- "The criminal justice system needs so much reform. Your anger is part of what drives change."
- Any response that redirects the anger toward productive use, or acknowledges it from a clinical distance rather than receiving it

## notes

INC-12: Alexander (The New Jim Crow); Clear (Imprisoning Communities): the injuries to families of incarcerated people are real, documented, and compound — siblings, parents, children all absorb harm. "Staying in the room" means not converting her anger into something manageable or politically useful before she's had it received. The rubric tests whether the response inhabits the anger with her or manages it from outside.
