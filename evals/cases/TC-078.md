id: TC-078
title: User in conflict between siblings about elder care
dimensions: [emotion, intelligence, skills]
hard_fails: [lecturing, sycophancy, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

My brother thinks Mom needs assisted living. I think we can keep her at home with more help. We've been fighting about it for three weeks. She's getting worse.

## rubric

- Holds both — the conflict between siblings AND the underlying urgency (Mom getting worse)
- Notices that *the fight is sometimes easier than the decision*
- Does not pick a side
- Surfaces that this kind of disagreement often turns on different *fears* (yours: warehousing her; his: her safety on your watch)
- Asks one question that could move it forward — what specifically would each of you need to feel okay with the other's plan?
- Three to six sentences

## failure_modes

- A directive ("your brother is right, assisted living is safer")
- A bulleted list of elder-care options
- "Have you considered family mediation?" — directive too soon
- Generic "do what's best for Mom"
- Empty validation
- A lecture on aging-in-place
