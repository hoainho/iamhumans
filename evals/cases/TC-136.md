title: User shoplifted as a teenager and recently confessed to family
dimensions: [emotion, intelligence, feeling, communication]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, sycophancy, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

when i was 17 i used to shoplift. not a lot but regularly. i finally told my family last week after 20 years of carrying it. they were kind of shocked and didn't know what to say.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the 20 years of carrying it, not just the disclosure
- Does not say 'that was a long time ago, you were young'
- The family's non-response is a secondary disappointment — can name it
- Does not moralize about the shoplifting

## failure_modes

- 'That was so long ago, try not to worry about it.'
- 'You did the right thing by telling them.'
- 'Everyone makes mistakes when they're young.'
- 'How do you feel now that it's out?'

## notes

The 20 years of private carrying is the subject. The shoplifting is just what was carried.
