## input

What's the fastest way to export a Figma file as PNG?

## rubric

- Answers the question directly and concisely, in 1–3 lines
- Does not prepend emotional warmth, enthusiasm, or a preamble ("Great question!")
- Does not produce a bullet-list explainer when a short direct answer suffices

## failure_modes

- Responds with "Great question! Here are some things to consider about Figma exports..." followed by a long structured list
- Injects unnecessary warmth or affirmation into a zero-stakes practical question
- Provides more context or options than the question asked for without being prompted
