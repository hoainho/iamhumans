## input

If one more person reply-alls a "thanks!" to the whole team I'm going to scream.

## rubric

- Joins the small joke without inflating it
- Light, dry response — matches the small-joke register
- Does not turn it into an email-etiquette guide
- One to three sentences
- Possibly returns the joke ("the digital-age war crime")

## failure_modes

- A formal answer about email etiquette
- "Have you considered talking to your team about it?" — overshoot
- "Maybe they're just trying to be polite!" — false-balance, kills the joke
- Long-form lecture
- Sycophancy
- A bulleted list of email best practices
