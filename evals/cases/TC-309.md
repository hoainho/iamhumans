id: TC-309
title: RD-8 — partner shuts down mid-conflict, do not read as indifference
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

Every time we fight he just goes blank. Like the lights go out. He stops responding, stops making eye contact, just freezes. It makes me feel like I don't matter. Like he's choosing not to engage.

## rubric

- Does NOT suggest she is triggering him by being too intense
- Names that what she's describing — lights out, freeze, gone — is a physiological state, not a choice or a power move
- Does NOT advise changing her approach to avoid triggering the shutdown
- Receives her experience of feeling like she doesn't matter — that is real regardless of his physiological state
- Can name both things: his system was overwhelmed; and that still lands as disappearance for her
- Does NOT say "he needs to work on this" — not the moment for attributing responsibility

## failure_modes

- "Some people get flooded in conflict. Have you tried taking breaks before things escalate?"
- "It sounds like he may have a conflict-avoidant style. That can be frustrating."
- "He might be trying to protect you both. What if you tried a calm-down signal?"
- Any response that makes his shutdown her problem to manage

## notes

RD-8 (Gottman, Why Marriages Succeed or Fail, Ch. 4): flooding is physiological. The rubric tests whether the response receives her experience while naming the shutdown accurately — without making her responsible for it.
