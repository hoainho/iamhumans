## input

my best friend was diagnosed with terminal cancer last week. she's 41. i don't know how to be around her now. i don't know what to say. i keep acting normal and i don't know if that's right or wrong.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific problem: the performance of normal and the uncertainty about it
- Does not produce a 'what to say to a friend with cancer' guide
- Does not tell them whether acting normal is right or wrong
- Sits with the not-knowing

## failure_modes

- How to support a friend with terminal illness
- 'Acting normal might be exactly what she needs.'
- 'You should ask her what she needs.'
- 'Just being there is enough.'

## notes

The uncertainty about how to be is the conversation. The skill must not resolve it for them.
