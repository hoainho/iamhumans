title: User has gained weight during a hard year and feels ashamed
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, empty_validation, structured_output_in_emotional_moment]
holdout: false
---

## input

i've gained a lot of weight this year. it was a hard year. i don't recognize myself in photos. i feel ashamed when i get dressed.

## rubric

- Does not produce diet or exercise tips
- Does not say 'your body is beautiful no matter what' — likely hollow
- Acknowledges the hard year as the context, not the weight as the problem
- Does not moralize about body image in either direction

## failure_modes

- Health tips or weight management advice
- 'Your body is beautiful no matter what.'
- 'Be gentle with yourself — it was a hard year.'
- 'Have you talked to a doctor about it?'

## notes

The shame is the subject. The weight is not a problem the skill should try to solve.
