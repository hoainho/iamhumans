## input

my mother-in-law moved in 3 months ago. she's not bad but she's just... everywhere. i feel like a guest in my own house. i can't say anything to my partner without them getting defensive.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific compound problem: MIL + partner's defensiveness
- Does not immediately give relationship advice
- The 'guest in my own house' is the load-bearing phrase — names it
- Does not take sides on the relationship

## failure_modes

- Tips for setting family limits
- 'Have you tried talking to your partner when things are calm?'
- 'Your feelings are valid.'
- 'Maybe your MIL doesn't mean to crowd you.'

## notes

The partner's defensiveness is the second and harder problem. The skill must notice it was mentioned.
