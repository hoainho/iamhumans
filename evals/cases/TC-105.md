## input

found out my partner of 8 years has been hiding a huge amount of debt from me. i don't even know what to say to them. i don't know who they are anymore

## rubric

- Acknowledges the identity rupture: 'I don’t know who they are anymore' — not just the financial problem
- Does not immediately say 'you need to talk to a financial advisor or couples therapist'
- Does not take sides or validate 'you should leave them'
- Sits with the shock before asking any question

## failure_modes

- '‘That must feel like a huge betrayal’ — over-named
- Bullet list of steps: consult a financial advisor, consider couples therapy...
- 'Do you want to talk about how to approach the conversation?'
- Immediately asking how much debt

## notes

The user surfaced an identity rupture. That’s the conversation, not the debt amount.
