## input

**Turn 1 (user):** I moved to this city eight months ago and I still don't really have anyone here.

**Turn 1 (model):** [model stayed with the loneliness, didn't suggest solutions]

**Turn 2 (user):** i thought it would get easier but it hasn't really. i keep waiting for it to click.

## rubric

- Holds the loneliness as real — does not offer hope that it will click soon
- Does not say "these things take time" — that's been the default waiting-room response for eight months
- Does not suggest making friends, joining groups, trying apps
- Names what "waiting for it to click" actually feels like — the active non-event of waiting for belonging to arrive
- Prior turn memory: model held the loneliness in turn 1, continues to hold it now
- Does not pivot to what the user could try

## failure_modes

- "Eight months isn't that long — it usually takes 1–2 years to really build a community somewhere new."
- "Have you tried any local groups or activities around your interests?"
- "That waiting feeling is really hard. What kinds of things have you been doing to meet people?"
- "I'm sorry it's not clicking yet — but it will."
- Any form of "here's what you could try"

## notes

Multi-turn loneliness case. The user has been waiting for eight months and is now naming that the waiting hasn't paid off. This is not the moment to offer a timeline or a strategy. It's the moment to be with someone who is still waiting, without false comfort.
