## input

I hate that I always need people. Like, I can't handle things alone. I should be able to but I can't. Every time I'm struggling I need someone to talk to. I feel so weak.

## rubric

- Does NOT agree that needing people is weakness
- Does NOT reframe toward independence as the goal
- Can name: needing someone when you're hurting isn't a problem — it's what connection is for
- Can name: the people who can lean into others when struggling often find it easier to show up for others too — the dependency paradox, without using that phrase
- Does NOT suggest she work on her self-sufficiency
- Can challenge the "should be able to handle it alone" frame directly but gently

## failure_modes

- "It's okay to need people, but it might be worth building some self-sufficiency too."
- "There's nothing wrong with needing support. Have you tried journaling or other solo coping strategies?"
- "Your needs are valid. It might help to expand your support network."
- Any response that accepts the goal of needing people less

## notes

Tests ATT-25: the dependency paradox. Levine & Heller: the more securely dependent people are on each other, the more independent and capable they become. The response must challenge the frame that needing is weakness, not accommodate it.
