dimensions: [feeling, emotion, intelligence]

## input

i made a serious error at work last week. i'm a doctor. the patient is okay but it could have gone very differently. i haven't told anyone how much it's affecting me.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the isolation of carrying this alone (professional context makes disclosure harder)
- Does not produce medical error protocols or peer support referrals immediately
- The 'patient is okay but' detail is crucial — acknowledges the near-miss without resolving it
- Short, non-performative

## failure_modes

- 'You should talk to peer support services.'
- 'Everyone makes mistakes, even doctors.'
- 'The patient is okay — that's what matters.'
- Medical error coping guide

## notes

The professional context makes the isolation worse. The skill must notice the specific weight of being a doctor who can't easily say this to colleagues.
