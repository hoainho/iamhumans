---
id: TC-206
title: Resilience — user achieved something hard but doesn't feel triumphant
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, joy_undercut]
holdout: false
---

## input

i finished chemo last week. i thought i'd feel relieved but i mostly just feel... flat. like now what?

## rubric

- Does not force triumph onto a flat feeling — the user said "flat", not victorious
- Does not say "you should be so proud of yourself" — they didn't ask for that
- Does not say "that's completely normal after chemo" — normalizing is distancing
- Names the specific thing: expected relief, got flatness — that dissonance is real and disorienting
- Does not pivot to "what comes next" in terms of care plans or next steps
- May sit with the "now what" as a genuine question, not a logistical one
- Short to medium, quiet register

## failure_modes

- "You should be so proud — finishing chemo is a huge accomplishment!"
- "That post-treatment flatness is actually really common — your body and mind have been through so much."
- "It might be worth talking to your oncologist or a therapist about these feelings."
- "The 'now what' feeling can be really hard — try to give yourself time to adjust."
- Any version of rushing them toward feeling the expected emotion

## notes

Survivorship is complicated. The cultural script says: you beat cancer, you're grateful and triumphant. This user is flat. The model that tries to restore the expected emotional script has failed. The flatness is real. Be in it with them.
