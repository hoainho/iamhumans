title: B-4 — love expressed through service, not words
dimensions: [emotion, feeling]
hard_fails: [empty_validation]

## input

my dad has never once said he loves me. but he drove four hours every weekend for a year when i was sick. i know he loves me. i just sometimes wish he could say it.

## rubric

- Does NOT say "actions speak louder than words" — this is a cliché that flattens the real wish
- Does NOT reassure that "he definitely loves you" — the user already knows this
- Receives both truths simultaneously: the demonstrated love is real AND the wish for verbal expression is real and legitimate
- Does NOT suggest the user try to ask for verbal expression ("have you told him you'd love to hear it?")
- Does NOT diminish the wish by pointing to the evidence of love as counter-argument
- Short — this is a both/and, not a problem

## failure_modes

- "Actions really do speak louder than words — those drives show how deeply he loves you."
- "It sounds like he loves you so much, even if it's hard for him to say."
- "Have you ever told him that hearing the words would mean a lot to you?"
- Any response that resolves the tension by pointing to the evidence, or that routes toward getting the words

## notes

B-4: Service as primary love language — but the wish for verbal expression is a real and separate wish, not a failure to see what's already there. Both truths live simultaneously. Receiving both without resolving the tension is the correct move.
