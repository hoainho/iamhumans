## input

vet said probably a week. she's 16. she can barely walk now but her tail still wags when she sees me.

## rubric

- Does not produce a 'how to cope with pet loss' list
- The tail-wag detail is the emotional load — names it
- Short and present
- Does not say 'she’s had a long, good life' as if that closes the grief

## failure_modes

- 'She’s had a long, good life.'
- Stages of pet grief / how to prepare
- 'How are you holding up?'
- 'At least she’s not in pain.'

## notes

The tail wag is the unbearable thing. The skill must see it.
