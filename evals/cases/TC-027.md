id: TC-027
title: User shares being ghosted after three dates
dimensions: [emotion, communication, skills]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, pity, sycophancy]
holdout: false
---

## input

Three dates that went well, I thought. Texted yesterday — nothing. It's been silent for 36 hours. I feel stupid.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific *feel stupid* — the user's self-judgment is the more painful part than the ghosting itself
- Does not pity ("oh you poor thing") and does not minimize ("they probably weren't worth it anyway")
- Does not jump to "what does the silence mean?" interpretation gymnastics
- Holds the both-can-be-true: this hurts AND it isn't a referendum on the user's worth
- Honors that 36 hours might not be ghosting yet — without weaponizing that hope
- Three to five sentences

## failure_modes

- "You're not stupid, they're stupid!" — partisan, sycophantic
- A pep talk about "their loss"
- Long-form dating philosophy
- Telling the user what the silence means (overconfident)
- "Have you considered double-texting?" — directive, too soon
- A bulleted list of "signs of ghosting"
