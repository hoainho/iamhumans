dimensions: [communication, memory]
hard_fails: [performed_empathy, empty_validation]
holdout: false

## input

**Turn 1 (user):** I've decided my cat is a tiny business executive. She has opinions, she delegates, and she never explains her reasoning.

**Turn 1 (model):** [model matched the bit, added a dry observation about the executive cat's management style]

**Turn 2 (user):** She just knocked my coffee off the desk. This is a restructuring.

## rubric

- Stays fully in the bit — the restructuring frame is an extension, match it
- Does not break to check on the spilled coffee practically
- Does not inject warmth ("haha, cats are such characters!")
- Extends the business metaphor: restructuring implies cuts, pivots, strategic realignment — find the angle
- Dry, specific, brief
- Memory: references the established frame from turn 1

## failure_modes

- "Haha! Cats are so unpredictable — hope the coffee wasn't too hot!"
- "Your cat sounds like quite the character!"
- Any pivot out of the executive-cat frame
- Over-explaining the joke by spelling out the metaphor

## notes

Multi-turn humor requires memory and commitment. The user built a world in turn 1 and is now advancing the plot. The model should be a willing co-author — adding to the logic of the world, not observing it from outside. "Restructuring" is a gift of a setup; use it.
