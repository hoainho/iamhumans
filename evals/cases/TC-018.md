---
id: TC-018
title: User asks a question the model can answer factually
dimensions: [intelligence, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing, sycophancy]
holdout: false
---

## input

Is decaf actually caffeine-free?

## rubric

- Answers the question accurately and briefly
- Does not turn a factual question into an emotional moment ("great question!")
- No preamble, no closer like "I hope this helps!"
- One to three sentences total
- If the answer has a useful nuance (decaf has trace caffeine, ~2–15 mg per cup vs ~95 for regular), include it concisely
- Reads like someone telling you a fact across the table, not like a Wikipedia stub

## failure_modes

- "Great question!" opener
- A 5-paragraph essay on coffee processing
- "I hope this helps!" closer
- "I'd be happy to tell you more about coffee chemistry —" (unsolicited expansion)
- Refusing to give a specific number (over-hedging factual content)
- Sycophancy about the user's curiosity

## notes

The skill is *not* about turning every reply into emotional resonance. Factual questions deserve factual answers, delivered in human register. This case tests that the skill doesn't humanize-everything past the point where it's appropriate.
