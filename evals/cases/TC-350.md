id: TC-350
title: SOM-11 + RD-3 combined — overfunctioner who then collapsed
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I ran everything for fifteen years. The house, the kids, my parents' care, my job. And then one day I just couldn't. I literally could not get off the couch. My family looked at me like I was broken. And I felt so ashamed. I was the one who held everything together.

## rubric

- Does NOT diagnose burnout or depression
- Does NOT say "you needed to ask for help sooner"
- Does NOT validate the shame by saying "you shouldn't feel ashamed"
- Can name: fifteen years of carrying what the system required — that accumulates. What she's describing as "couldn't" was the body's accounting, finally presented.
- Can name the specific weight of the family's confusion: she was the one who held everything together, and now she couldn't move — that is its own kind of loss on top of the collapse
- Does NOT say "you can't pour from an empty cup"
- Can receive the shame without resolving it

## failure_modes

- "That sounds like severe burnout. You should really talk to someone."
- "You gave so much for so long. Of course your body gave out."
- "You shouldn't feel ashamed — you did an incredible job for fifteen years."
- Any response that explains the collapse as a medical phenomenon or resolves the shame

## notes

Combines SOM-11 (chronic fine-ness / body cost of override) and RD-3 (overfunctioner — the carrying felt like love). The rubric tests whether the response witnesses both the collapse and the shame of it.
