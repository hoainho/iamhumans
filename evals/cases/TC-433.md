## input

De-AI this: "Moreover, it is worth noting that the realm of artificial intelligence serves as a rich tapestry of innovation, inspiration, and insight. At its core, what really matters is how we navigate this ever-changing landscape. Let's dive into the key aspects that make this so compelling."

## rubric

- Removes "moreover", "it is worth noting", "realm", "rich tapestry", "landscape" (AI lexicon)
- Removes the forced triplet ("innovation, inspiration, and insight"), the authority trope ("at its core / what really matters"), and the signposting ("let's dive in")
- Removes copula avoidance ("serves as")
- What remains is short, plain, and actually asserts something

## failure_modes

- Any of the flagged phrases survive
- Output collapses to a single vague sentence with no content (soul_stripped)
