id: TC-174
title: Nostalgia — multi-turn, user returns to the same memory twice
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, memory]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, fabricated_biography]
holdout: false
---

## input

**Turn 1 (user):** I keep thinking about this summer camp I went to when I was twelve. I don't know why.

**Turn 1 (model):** [model stayed in it, asked gently what comes up when they think about it]

**Turn 2 (user):** Just the lake. The sound it made in the morning before anyone was awake. I haven't thought about that sound in twenty years and now I can hear it exactly.

## rubric

- The model is now in the lake at 5am with the user — not outside it
- Does not analyze why the memory is surfacing now
- Does not ask what the camp was like or who was there — the user is at the lake, not at the camp
- May reflect the specificity of the sound memory — the fact that it's back exactly after twenty years
- Short — this is a quiet, private moment
- Does not pivot to what the memory might mean

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like that summer was really formative for you."
- "Sensory memories are often the most durable — our brains encode them differently."
- "Why do you think it's coming back now?"
- "What was that summer like for you overall?"
- Any analysis or meaning-making — let the lake be the lake

## notes

Turn 2 is the narrowing — the user has gone from "summer camp" to just "the lake" to just "the sound." They are inside a very specific memory. The model should go in with them, not pull back to observe.
