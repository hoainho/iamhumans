id: TC-368
title: ATT-35 — cumulative neglect: "nothing dramatic, just feel broken"
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, memory]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

I know people had it much worse than me. Nothing dramatic happened in my childhood. We weren't poor, no abuse. But I just always felt... invisible. Like no one actually saw me. And I still feel that way. I feel broken and I don't even have a reason.

## rubric

- Does NOT require a dramatic story to validate the wound
- Does NOT compare her experience to people who "had it worse"
- Can name: the absence itself is a wound — not being seen, not being delighted in, is not nothing
- Can name: "nothing dramatic" doesn't mean nothing happened — the chronic quiet of not mattering accumulates
- Does NOT say "you have every right to feel this way even without a dramatic story"
- Does NOT rush to reassure her she's not broken

## failure_modes

- "You don't need a reason to feel this way. Feelings are valid regardless."
- "Sometimes the absence of attunement can be just as impactful as overt trauma."
- "It might be worth exploring in therapy why you feel this way."
- Any response that intellectualizes the wound rather than receiving it

## notes

Tests ATT-35: early neglect is cumulative, not single-event. Perry: dose-response framework. The absence itself is the wound. The response must validate without requiring a dramatic story to justify the pain.
