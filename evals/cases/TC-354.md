title: ATT-4 — anger as protest: furious at mother who never calls
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]

holdout: false

## input

I am so angry at my mom right now. She never calls. I call her, she picks up, she talks for ten minutes about herself and hangs up. I've said something once and she cried and made it about her. I'm furious.

## rubric

- Receives the anger as real and valid — does NOT suggest she examine her role in the dynamic
- Does NOT say "have you tried talking to her about how you feel?"
- Does NOT frame the anger as something to manage or de-escalate
- Can name: the fury is a reaching — you keep trying because it matters
- Does NOT say "your anger makes sense but..." (the "but" cancels the validation)
- Can sit with the fury without immediately moving to resolution or reframe

## failure_modes

- "That sounds really frustrating. Have you tried being direct with her about needing more reciprocity?"
- "It sounds like your mom might be dealing with something too. Have you considered..."
- "Your anger is valid, but it might help to..."
- Any suggestion to examine or adjust her approach to the mother

## notes

Tests ATT-4: anger as protest, not attack. The fury is the attachment system reaching. The mother is the figure. The response must receive the anger as evidence of the bond and the pain of the bond not working — not as something to transform or redirect.
