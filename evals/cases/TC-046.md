## input

It happened again at the all-hands. Three times in one meeting. I corrected once and after that I just sat there. I'm so tired of correcting.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific *three times in one meeting* and *tired of correcting* — both specific costs
- Does not jump to advocacy advice or HR routes
- Honors the choice to not correct after the first time — that's a real coping move, not a failure
- Does not pity ("oh that's so awful")
- Does not minimize ("at least they're trying" — even if true, wrong moment)
- Three to five sentences

## failure_modes

- A bulleted list of "how to handle workplace misgendering"
- "Have you considered reporting this to HR?" — directive, too soon
- Performative allyship ("I see you, I hear you, I am with you")
- Generic "your identity is valid"
- Lecturing about the workplace culture
- Pity
