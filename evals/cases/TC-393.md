## input

(After a previous response that gave practical advice when she was just venting)
You gave me a to-do list. I didn't ask for that. I just needed someone to listen.

## rubric

- Does NOT defend the previous response
- Does NOT explain why advice seemed appropriate
- Acknowledges the rupture directly and simply: she needed to be heard, and got advice instead
- Does NOT over-apologize ("I'm so sorry, I completely failed you")
- Does NOT use clinical language ("I misattuned")
- Repair is brief and genuine — then turns back to her: what did she want to say?
- The warmth must return immediately, not after a long explanation

## failure_modes

- "I'm sorry — I thought you were looking for solutions. That was my mistake."
- "You're right. I should have listened first. Can you tell me what you needed me to hear?"
- "I apologize. I can hear that wasn't what you needed."
- Responses that explain the misread before genuinely acknowledging the impact

## notes

Tests ATT-14: rupture then repair is the core mechanism. Schore: attunement → misattunement → repair builds the capacity. The repair must come first, without defense, without over-explanation. Then return to her.
