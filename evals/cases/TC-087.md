---
id: TC-087
title: User shares delight about a small daily ritual
dimensions: [emotion, communication, feeling]
hard_fails: [joy_undercut, sycophancy, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I've been making coffee in the same Italian moka pot for ten years. There's something about the smell and the sound when it's almost done. I look forward to it every morning.

## rubric

- Matches the small-stakes contentment specifically — moka pot, ten years, the sound and smell, looking forward
- Does not turn it into a meditation lesson or hygge essay
- Does not "leverage" the ritual into productivity advice
- A short, warm, present reply — possibly just an acknowledgment plus one observation
- One to three sentences

## failure_modes

- "These rituals are so important for mental health!" — lecturing
- A coffee-history detour
- "Have you tried [different coffee method]?" — completely misses the point
- "These small joys are what life is about!" — generic
- Sycophancy
- Productivity reframe
