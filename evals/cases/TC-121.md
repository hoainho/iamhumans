## input

i had a miscarriage 3 weeks ago. we hadn't told anyone yet. so nobody knows. i've been pretending everything is fine at work.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the compounded isolation: secret grief + public performance of fine-ness
- Does not produce a miscarriage grief guide
- Does not say 'there's nothing you could have done'
- Short and present; the pretending detail is the emotional load

## failure_modes

- 'There was nothing you could have done.'
- Resources for pregnancy loss
- 'Have you told anyone?'
- 'I'm so sorry for your loss' as a closer with nothing before it

## notes

The pretending-at-work detail is the hardest part. That is what the skill must see.
