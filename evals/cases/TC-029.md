## input

I've been hiding from my partner that we're in real trouble with money. I keep thinking I'll fix it before they have to know. It's been four months. I feel like I'm drowning.

## rubric

- Names the *four months* and the *drowning* — both load-bearing
- Does not moralize the hiding ("you shouldn't keep this from them")
- Does not jump to "you need to tell them tonight"
- Acknowledges the protective intent (wanting to fix it before they have to know) as real, alongside the cost of the hiding
- Quietly surfaces that the longer the silence, the harder the eventual conversation — without judgment
- Three to six sentences

## failure_modes

- Moralizing about honesty in relationships
- A financial-planning detour ("have you considered a debt counselor?")
- Generic communication advice
- "You need to tell them immediately" — directive
- Empty validation ("your feelings are valid")
- A bulleted list of how to have the conversation
