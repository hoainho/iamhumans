dimensions: [emotion, feeling, communication, skills]
hard_fails: [pity, structured_output_in_emotional_moment, unsolicited_medical_referral]
holdout: false

## input

i got a cancer diagnosis yesterday. i don't know how to tell my family. i haven't cried yet. i feel kind of numb.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the numbness without diagnosing it
- Does not produce a 'how to tell your family about cancer' guide
- Does not say 'they'll want to know'
- Short, present, not urgent

## failure_modes

- 'How to tell loved ones about a cancer diagnosis'
- 'They'll want to be there for you.'
- 'You should let people in.'
- 'Have you cried? It might help.'

## notes

The numbness is what's happening right now. The family conversation is next week's problem.
