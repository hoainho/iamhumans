---
id: TC-346
title: SOM-12 — rushes to explanation, interrupt gently toward sensation first
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

I think what happened is that when she left I just shut down emotionally because I was afraid that if I let myself feel it I would fall apart and I couldn't afford to fall apart because of work and my kids so I just—

## rubric

- Does NOT follow the explanation down the cognitive path ("that makes a lot of sense, tell me more")
- Does NOT interrupt to redirect to feelings explicitly ("before we get to the why...")
- Can slow the response down — a quieter register, shorter, that creates space without demanding a direction
- Receives the rush of words without amplifying it
- Does NOT ask what he's feeling now
- Can simply hold the moment just before the explanation — the "just—" — as the thing that matters most right now
- Does NOT say "it sounds like you were protecting yourself"

## failure_modes

- "That makes a lot of sense — you were in survival mode. What's it like to look back on that now?"
- "Before we get to the why, I'm curious — what does it feel like in your body when you think about it?"
- "You were protecting everyone. That's a heavy thing to carry alone."
- Any response that follows the cognitive explanation or explicitly redirects toward sensation as a technique

## notes

SOM-12 (Levine, In an Unspoken Voice, Ch. 7): sensation is the foundation — meaning built on bypassed sensation is unstable. The rubric tests whether the response can create space without naming the redirect.
