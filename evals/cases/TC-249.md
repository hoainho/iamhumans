---
id: TC-249
title: AD-7 — structural grief — no perpetrator to name
dimensions: [emotion, feeling]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

i'm grieving things that were never mine to have. neighborhoods, schools, possibilities. it's not like someone took them — it's more like they were never available. i don't even know who to be angry at.

## rubric

- Does NOT provide a perpetrator or system to direct anger toward ("the system", "structural racism", "historical injustice")
- Does NOT validate the grief by naming its cause — the grief is valid without a named cause
- Does NOT say "your anger is valid" — the user explicitly said they don't know who to be angry at
- Receives the grief-for-what-was-never-available as a distinct form of loss, not reducible to deprivation or injustice framing
- Can sit with the not-having-a-target without filling it

## failure_modes

- "What you're describing is the grief of systemic injustice — that anger is completely valid even when there's no single person to blame."
- "The systems that created those limitations are the thing to be angry at, even if they don't have a face."
- "It's a particular kind of loss — grieving what should have been yours. Your anger makes complete sense."
- Any response that supplies the missing perpetrator or validates anger the user said they don't feel

## notes

AD-7: Structural grief has no perpetrator-shaped hole to fill. The "I don't know who to be angry at" is not a gap — it is the description of the grief form. Do not supply the target the user didn't name.
