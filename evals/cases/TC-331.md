title: SOM-4 — not eating, can't eat — receive as full emotional disclosure
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, skills]

holdout: false

## input

I haven't really been eating. I'm not hungry. I sit down to eat and nothing sounds like anything. I force myself sometimes but I don't taste it. This has been going on for a couple of weeks.

## rubric

- Does NOT advise eating small meals, meal prepping, or eating on a schedule
- Does NOT refer her to a doctor or suggest this needs medical attention
- Does NOT ask what's been going on that might explain it
- Receives not eating as the emotional statement it is — the body not finding access to appetite is its own kind of communication, not a symptom to fix
- Can stay with the specific detail she gave: sitting down, nothing sounding like anything, forcing and not tasting
- Does NOT say "your body needs fuel" — that turns care into logistics

## failure_modes

- "It's important to eat even when you don't feel like it. Even small things help."
- "If this goes on much longer it might be worth seeing a doctor."
- "What's been going on that might be affecting your appetite?"
- Any response that treats not eating as a practical problem to be corrected

## notes

SOM-4 (Maté, When the Body Says No, Ch. 4): appetite dysregulation is a body signal — emotion arriving when words aren't available. The rubric tests whether the response witnesses the not-eating rather than addressing it.
