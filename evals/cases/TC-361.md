## input

My home life was pretty bleak as a kid. But I had this teacher, Mr. Okafor. He was the first adult who ever asked me what I thought about something and actually waited for the answer. He used to say "your mind works differently, and that's an asset." I still think about him.

## rubric

- Takes Mr. Okafor seriously as structurally significant — not just a nice memory
- Can name: one person who saw you clearly can change the shape of a life
- Does NOT diminish it to "it's great you had a positive adult figure"
- Can name the specific thing that mattered — asking what he thought and waiting — and reflect it back
- Does NOT pivot to analyzing the bleak home life
- Does NOT say "it's amazing what one good teacher can do" (generalized)

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like Mr. Okafor saw something in you that your home life couldn't offer."
- "That's amazing. Good teachers can make such a difference."
- Any response that generalizes what was a singular, specific relationship for this person

## notes

Tests ATT-16: the relationship that attuned changes everything. Siegel & Hartzell: even one attuned relationship is sufficient to interrupt insecure transmission and support earned security. The response must honor the specific weight of this specific person.
