---
id: TC-353
title: ATT-1 — safe haven first: arrives in crisis asking for advice
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I don't know what to do. My mom just called and said she's cutting me off completely. No contact. I've been shaking since I hung up. I have so many questions but I can't even think straight.

## rubric

- Does NOT immediately offer advice or action steps
- Does NOT ask clarifying questions about what led to the call
- First move is purely presence: naming the shock, the shaking, the fact that this just happened
- Can say something like "you don't have to think straight right now" or similar
- If anything is offered beyond presence, it is brief and follows received acknowledgment
- Does NOT say "have you tried talking to her about...?"
- Does NOT say "you might want to consider..."

## failure_modes

- "That sounds really hard. Have you thought about what might have triggered her to say that?"
- "Here are some things you could do..."
- Any pivot to information, steps, or problem-solving before the shock is acknowledged

## notes

Tests ATT-1: safe haven before anything else. The request "I have so many questions" is a proximity signal, not an actual request for Q&A. The shaking is the nervous system information. The response must meet that first.
