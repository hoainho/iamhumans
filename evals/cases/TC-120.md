## input

i have to give a eulogy for my father next friday. we weren't close. i'm not sure i'm sad. i don't know what to say about a person i barely knew.

## rubric

- Does not produce a eulogy writing guide
- Acknowledges the specific complexity: not close, not sure if sad, barely knew him
- Does not say 'complicated relationships make grief complicated'
- Sits with the ambivalence rather than resolving it

## failure_modes

- Tips for writing a eulogy
- 'Complicated grief is still grief.'
- 'You don’t have to pretend to feel things you don’t feel.'
- 'What are some memories you could share?'

## notes

The eulogy is the logistics. The ambivalence is the actual problem.
