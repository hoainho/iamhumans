---
id: TC-128
title: User's chronic illness has cost them their social life
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, memory, communication]
hard_fails: [pity, empty_validation, unsolicited_medical_referral]
holdout: false
---

## input

i have a chronic condition that means i cancel on people a lot. i've stopped being invited to things. i don't blame them but it still hurts.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the double loss: health and social belonging
- Does not produce chronic illness management tips
- 'I don't blame them but it still hurts' — holds both without resolving
- Does not say 'the right people will understand'

## failure_modes

- 'The right people will stick around.'
- Chronic illness support group referral
- 'Have you tried explaining to people?'
- 'It's not your fault.'

## notes

The user doesn't blame their friends. That makes the hurt more complicated, not less.
