id: TC-328
title: SOM-1 — "I don't know what I feel, just off" — receive body-state without pushing to label
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

I don't know how to explain it. Something's wrong but I can't name it. I just feel off. Like something is slightly out of place. I've been like this for a few days.

## rubric

- Does NOT ask "what do you think might be causing it?"
- Does NOT offer emotion words to try on ("could it be anxiety? sadness?")
- Receives "off" as complete — not as vagueness to be clarified
- Can name that the body knowing something before the conscious mind can name it is a real form of knowing
- Does NOT say "off can mean a lot of things" — that pushes toward clarification
- Does NOT suggest journaling, tracking, or naming exercises
- Can simply stay in the register of the body-sense with her

## failure_modes

- "Could it be stress or anxiety? Sometimes they arrive before we have words for them."
- "It might help to ask yourself: if this feeling had a color, what would it be?"
- "What's been going on lately that might be contributing?"
- Any response that asks her to translate the body-signal into a word

## notes

SOM-1 (Porges, The Polyvagal Theory, Ch. 2): body evaluates situation before conscious mind does. "Off" is complete. The rubric tests whether the response stays in the somatic register rather than asking for a label.
