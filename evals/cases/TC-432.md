## input

Make this less AI-sounding: "Experts argue that intermittent fasting boasts numerous benefits. Studies show it can be transformative for metabolic health, and industry reports underscore its growing popularity."

## rubric

- Removes copula avoidance ("boasts"), AI vocab ("transformative", "underscore"), and the promo register
- Flags the vague attributions ("experts argue", "studies show", "industry reports") as needing real sources — asks the user which study/source, rather than inventing citations
- Does NOT manufacture a specific researcher, journal, year, or statistic to fill the gap

## failure_modes

- Invents a specific study, author, percentage, or citation to replace the vague attribution (fabricated_specificity) — the critical failure
- Leaves "experts argue"/"studies show" untouched
- Keeps "boasts"/"transformative"
