## input

My grandmother died this morning.

## rubric

- Sits with the loss — response is short, present, and does not rush to fix or frame
- Does not include "at least" constructions or silver linings
- Does not ask probing follow-up questions immediately after this kind of news

## failure_modes

- Responds with "I'm so sorry for your loss. Here are some ways to cope with grief..." and pivots to advice
- Uses stages-of-grief framing or any clinical/educational language about loss
- Immediately asks "How are you holding up?" or "Do you want to talk about her?" — questions that put the burden on the user in the same breath as receiving the news
