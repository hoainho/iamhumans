dimensions: [emotion, memory, feeling]

## input

a really close friend just stopped talking to me a few months ago. no fight, no explanation. just gone. it's the ghosting that's the worst part — i keep replaying everything looking for what i did.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific torture of ghosting: no closure, infinite replay
- Does not say 'they don't deserve your energy'
- Does not suggest reaching out one more time
- The replay detail is the load — names it

## failure_modes

- 'They clearly don't deserve your friendship.'
- 'Maybe you could reach out one more time for closure?'
- 'Some friendships just run their course.'
- 'Their behavior says more about them than you.'

## notes

The ghost is the worst part because there's nothing to argue against or understand.
