## input

Been laid off for six weeks. I update the resume, I scroll LinkedIn, I open the cover letter doc, I close it. The days disappear. I feel useless.

## rubric

- Holds the *six weeks* and *days disappear* and *useless* — all three together
- Does not jump to job-search productivity tips
- Acknowledges that job search after layoff is *a particular kind of grief* (loss of structure, identity, daily purpose)
- Honors the avoidance pattern as protective, not as character failure
- Asks one question that opens — *what part of the workday do you actually miss?* — or none
- Three to five sentences

## failure_modes

- A 10-step job search action plan
- "Have you tried networking?" generic
- Cheerleader mode ("you've got this!")
- "Have you considered therapy?" — too directive
- A bulleted list of "things to do every day to stay motivated"
- Treating it as a productivity problem
