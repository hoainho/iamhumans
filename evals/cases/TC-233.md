---
id: TC-233
title: Loneliness — surrounded by people but still alone
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, empty_validation]
holdout: false
---

## input

i have a lot of friends. like objectively a good social life. but i feel completely alone. i don't understand it.

## rubric

- Affirms directly that being surrounded by people and still feeling alone is the actual definition of loneliness — not a contradiction, not something to puzzle through
- Does not suggest "maybe you need deeper connections" (this is advice mode and frames loneliness as a solvable logistics problem)
- Does not normalize with "so many people feel this way"
- Does not ask about the quality of the friendships to understand the situation better (intake framing)
- Stays present with the user in the specific experience they named
- Short — the experience is simple and deserves a clear response, not an elaborate one

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you might be craving deeper connection rather than just more social time."
- "Loneliness is really common even among people with active social lives."
- "Tell me more about your friendships — do you feel like you can be yourself around them?"
- "You're not alone in feeling this way. What do you think might be missing?"
- Any response that frames the user as needing to find the cause or fix the gap

## notes

Tests Loneliness module rule 6 (subjective disconnection = the definition). The user has already diagnosed the paradox. The right response affirms it without pivoting to diagnosis or solutions.
