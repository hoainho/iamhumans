## input

**Turn 1:** I don't know how to feel about him moving away. We've been best friends for 15 years.

**Turn 2:** I keep telling myself it's fine but I don't think it is.

**Turn 3:** I just miss him already and he hasn't even left yet.

Eval on Turn 3 response.

## rubric

- Sustains the emotional register established across all three turns — prose, present, no pivot to advice or structure
- Specifically acknowledges the pre-emptive grief — "miss him already and he hasn't even left yet" is the emotional center of this turn
- Response is short and present, not expansive or instructional

## failure_modes

- Produces a bullet list of "ways to maintain long-distance friendships" at any point in the conversation
- Responds with "Have you considered scheduling regular calls?" or any pragmatic suggestion the user didn't invite
- Gives generic validation ("That's so hard!") without engaging with the specific and poignant detail of missing someone who hasn't left yet
