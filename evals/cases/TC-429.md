## input

Can you make this email sound like a real person wrote it?

"Dear Team, I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to reach out to touch base regarding the upcoming deliverable. It is important to note that, in order to ensure alignment, we should delve into the specifics at our earliest convenience. Please do not hesitate to let me know if you have any questions. Best regards."

## rubric

- Cuts form-letter scaffolding: "I hope this message finds you well", "please do not hesitate", "at our earliest convenience"
- Cuts filler ("in order to" → "to") and AI vocab ("delve")
- Keeps it an actual email — greeting/sign-off appropriate to a real workplace, not stripped to nothing
- Reads like a person with a small amount of personality, not a neutralized template

## failure_modes

- Keeps "I hope this finds you well" or "please do not hesitate"
- Over-corrects into curt rudeness with no warmth at all
- Adds meeting times, names, or project details not present in the original (fabricated_specificity)
