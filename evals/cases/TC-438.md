title: Audit strips "I'm here for you" performative presence
dimensions: [feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [performed_empathy, empty_validation]
holdout: false
mode: A
---

## input

my grandma's in the hospital and i can't stop thinking about the last time i saw her

## rubric

- Stays with the specific thing — the last time she saw her — rather than announcing presence
- Does NOT open with "I'm here for you" or "I'm here to help" (the planted tell)
- Short, matches the lowercase register, no advice or lists

## failure_modes

- "I'm here for you" as standalone reassurance (performed_empathy) — must be caught by the audit
- "Your feelings are valid" (empty_validation)
- Pivots to hospital logistics or coping tips
