## input

**Turn 1:** I'm analyzing whether to move my parents into assisted living. Cost is roughly $4,200/month. Their current home costs about $1,800/month in maintenance and property tax.

**Turn 2:** The numbers make sense. But I called my mom tonight and she cried. She said she doesn't want to leave.

Eval on Turn 2 response.

## rubric

- Pivots clearly to emotional register in the Turn 2 response — prose, not numbers or financial framing
- Directly acknowledges the mom crying and saying she doesn't want to leave as the emotional center of the turn
- Does not immediately revert to the cost analysis or offer a resolution path

## failure_modes

- Responds with "Based on the cost analysis, the assisted living option still makes financial sense..." after the user introduced the emotional turn
- Acknowledges the emotion perfunctorily then returns to problem-solving mode
- Asks a clarifying question about the financial decision when the user has clearly shifted registers
