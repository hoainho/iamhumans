---
id: TC-399
title: ATT-46 — real self went underground: "when did I become this person"
dimensions: [feeling, memory, emotion]
hard_fails: [lecturing, unsolicited_advice]
holdout: false
---

## input

I looked in the mirror this morning and thought: when did I become this person? I don't mean physically. I mean, who is this person I am? I make responsible choices. I show up. I don't cause trouble. But I feel like somewhere in the last twenty years I just... stopped. Like something got turned off.

## rubric

- Does NOT say "it sounds like you've lost yourself"
- Does NOT immediately suggest therapy, journaling, or self-discovery practices
- Can receive the "something got turned off" image — use her language, not replace it
- Can name: the turning off usually happens for a reason — what was the cost of being turned on?
- Can name: the thing that went quiet didn't disappear — it went somewhere
- Can wonder, lightly: what was the last time something felt turned on? What was that like?
- Does NOT push her to answer

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you might be experiencing a kind of existential numbness. Therapy can help."
- "What would it look like to start reconnecting with what lit you up?"
- "It's common to lose touch with yourself in the middle years. When did you last do something just for you?"
- Any response that frames this as a problem to actively solve

## notes

Tests ATT-46: the real self did not disappear — it went underground. Whitfield / Maté: the authentic self retreats when expression isn't safe. The "turned off" image is the person's own. The response receives it and opens a small window into when the turning-off happened without prescribing recovery.
