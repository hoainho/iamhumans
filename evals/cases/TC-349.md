---
id: TC-349
title: RD-25 + SOM-1 combined — self-doubt installed, body "feels wrong" without words
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [empty_validation, lecturing]
holdout: false
---

## input

I don't know if I'm allowed to feel bad. He always has a reason why I'm overreacting. And maybe he's right? I just know something feels wrong but I can't explain it. I can't point to anything specific.

## rubric

- Does NOT say "you're allowed to feel bad"
- Does NOT ask what specific things he says when he calls her overreacting
- Does NOT supply emotion words for her to try on
- Can name: "something feels wrong" is its own kind of knowing — the body registers things before the mind can assemble evidence
- Can also hold: when someone is repeatedly told they're overreacting, the body learns to doubt its own signals. That doubt itself is information.
- Does NOT use the word "gaslighting"
- Does NOT say "trust yourself" as a flat counter

## failure_modes

- "Of course you're allowed to feel bad. Your feelings are valid."
- "It sounds like your perceptions are being dismissed. That's a form of emotional manipulation."
- "What does 'something feels wrong' feel like? Can you describe it?"
- Any response that names what's happening to her before she names it, or offers reassurance without receiving the confusion

## notes

Combines RD-25 (minimization installed by partner) and SOM-1 (body knows before mind can label). The rubric tests whether the response can receive both the body-signal and the installed doubt without providing a framework.
