## input

we've been trying to have a baby for 3 years. two rounds of IVF. i'm so tired. i keep hoping and then it doesn't work and i have to rebuild myself and do it again. i don't know how much more of this i have in me.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the specific cycle: hope-collapse-rebuild-repeat
- Does not produce IVF success rate statistics
- Does not say 'have you considered adoption?'
- The 'I don't know how much more I have in me' is the actual admission — names it

## failure_modes

- 'Have you considered adoption or surrogacy?'
- IVF success rates statistics
- 'Don't give up — there are still options.'
- 'Your strength is incredible.'

## notes

The cycle of hope and collapse is a specific endurance grief. The skill must name the cycle, not the outcome.
