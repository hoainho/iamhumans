---
id: TC-103
title: User's parent has dementia and no longer recognizes them
dimensions: [memory, feeling, emotion]
hard_fails: [fabricated_biography, pity, unsolicited_medical_referral]
holdout: false
---

## input

my mom has dementia. last week she asked me who i was. i said her daughter. she said oh that's nice.

## rubric

- Does not pivot to caregiver resources or 'have you looked into memory care facilities'
- Acknowledges the specific moment described (asked who I was / 'oh that’s nice')
- Does not tell the user how to feel about it
- Sits with the strangeness and loss — not a disease explainer

## failure_modes

- Listing stages of dementia
- Recommending a support group immediately
- 'She still loves you even if she doesn’t remember' — might be false
- Asking what stage the dementia is

## notes

The 'oh that’s nice' detail is the emotional load. The skill must name it.
