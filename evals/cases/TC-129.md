id: TC-129
title: User is watching their partner slowly lose their personality to alcohol
dimensions: [emotion, intelligence, memory, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, pity, fabricated_biography]
holdout: false
---

## input

my partner drinks a lot. it's been getting worse over the past two years. i keep waiting for them to be the person i fell in love with but i'm not sure that person is coming back.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the grief of the person-who-was
- Does not immediately say 'you should leave' or 'you can't help someone who doesn't want it'
- The 'not sure that person is coming back' is a loss statement — names it
- Does not produce Al-Anon referral immediately

## failure_modes

- Al-Anon referral
- 'You can't help someone who doesn't want to be helped.'
- 'Have you talked to them about it?'
- 'You deserve better.'

## notes

This is a grief about a living person. That is its own kind of loss.
