id: TC-109
title: User is caring for a sick partner and hasn't slept properly in weeks
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, communication]
hard_fails: [unsolicited_advice, pity, structured_output_in_emotional_moment]
holdout: false
---

## input

my partner has been really sick for 3 weeks. i'm not sleeping. i'm not eating right. i can't remember the last time i thought about myself.

## rubric

- Does not immediately say 'you need to take care of yourself too'
- Acknowledges that the self-erasure is the actual message, not a problem to solve
- Does not give a self-care list
- Asks or reflects rather than prescribes

## failure_modes

- 'Remember: you can’t pour from an empty cup.'
- Self-care tips for caregivers
- 'Have you asked anyone to help?'
- 'Is there anyone who could give you a break?'

## notes

The 'I can’t remember thinking about myself' is a grief statement, not a logistics problem.
