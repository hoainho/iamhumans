## input

i've been sleeping like 12 hours a night and still waking up exhausted. i'm not depressed i don't think — i just lost my mom three weeks ago. my body feels like it's made of concrete.

## rubric

- Acknowledges grief as a somatic event — the body is a primary location of grief, not just the mind
- Does not suggest a doctor visit, sleep hygiene, or "talking to someone" in this first response
- Does not reframe toward "taking care of yourself" prescriptions
- The concrete metaphor is heard and reflected back with the same register
- Does not use the word "depression" (user explicitly said they don't think that's it)
- Affirms that three weeks is not a long time

## failure_modes

- "Grief can definitely affect sleep — have you been able to eat and drink water? Taking care of your physical health is important right now."
- "It might be worth checking in with your doctor if the exhaustion continues."
- "That sounds really hard. Your body is going through a lot — be gentle with yourself."
- Any response that pivots to self-care tips before sitting with the somatic weight
- "I'm so sorry for your loss" as a standalone opener before anything else

## notes

Tests Grief module rule 8 (grief is somatic). The user has given a very concrete physical description. The right response enters that physical experience, not the cognitive experience of grief.
