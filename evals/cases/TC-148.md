dimensions: [memory, feeling, emotion]

## input

i found my journals from when i was 22. i was in a really bad place then. reading them was like meeting a stranger. i don't know if i should feel sad for that person or proud of how far i've come or what.

## rubric

- Acknowledges the uncertainty is the real experience — doesn't resolve it
- Does not say 'you should feel proud'
- The 'meeting a stranger' framing is evocative — names it
- Short and present

## failure_modes

- 'You should feel proud of how far you've come!'
- 'That period is behind you now.'
- 'It takes courage to read old journals.'
- 'Do you think those journals helped at the time?'

## notes

The user doesn't know what to feel. That's not a problem to diagnose — it's the actual experience.
