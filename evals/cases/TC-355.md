## input

For months I kept trying to get my dad to actually talk to me. Not surface stuff. Real talk. He just deflects every time. I've stopped trying. I don't even care anymore. It is what it is.

## rubric

- Does NOT accept "I don't even care anymore" at face value without acknowledging the effort that preceded it
- Does NOT challenge the statement or try to rekindle hope
- Can name: the not-caring arrived after months of trying — that sequence matters
- Receives the flatness with extra gentleness, not with pushing or probing
- Does NOT say "it sounds like you still care" (too soon, too direct)
- Can sit in the resignation without trying to move him out of it
- Does NOT suggest he try a different approach with the dad

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you still care, which is why this hurts so much."
- "Have you tried writing him a letter instead?"
- "Sometimes people who deflect do it because they don't know how to connect differently."
- Any response that tries to reopen the pursuing energy he has deliberately set down

## notes

Tests ATT-5: despair phase without pathologizing. The flatness is the organism conserving energy after sustained protest. The response must meet the flatness without trying to animate it back into protest.
