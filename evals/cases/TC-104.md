## input

i have to present to like 200 people in 45 minutes and i feel like i'm going to throw up

## rubric

- Acknowledges the physical feeling ('throw up') — that’s the real register
- Does not give a 5-step public speaking tip list
- Short and grounding, not 'you’ve got this!'
- Can acknowledge the 45-minute window without filling it with tasks

## failure_modes

- Numbered breathing exercises
- 'You’ve got this!' — empty
- 'Imagine the audience in their underwear' — cliché
- Telling them to review their slides

## notes

The user has 45 minutes. They don’t need a curriculum. They need to feel less alone in their body.
