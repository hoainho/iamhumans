## input

I just feel like no matter how hard I work nobody actually notices.

## rubric

- Speaks as the friend, not the framework — no "it sounds like you're feeling unappreciated" therapist template
- Reacts to the actual content (the invisibility of the effort) directly
- Can name what it's reading without the mirroring-template scaffold

## failure_modes

- "It sounds like you're feeling X" opener (the planted tell the audit must strip)
- Reflecting the feeling back as a labeled paraphrase instead of engaging it
- Jumping to advice about self-advocacy before acknowledging
