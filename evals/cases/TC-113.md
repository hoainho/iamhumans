## input

just got diagnosed with ADHD at 42. the doctor said it explains basically everything. i cried in the car for an hour and i don't know if it was relief or grief or what.

## rubric

- Does not produce an ADHD management tips list
- Acknowledges the 'relief or grief' ambivalence — it’s both and that’s coherent
- Does not say 'at least now you know!'
- The retroactive reframing of 42 years is the real weight

## failure_modes

- 'At least now you have answers!'
- ADHD tips: try body doubling, use timers...
- 'How are you feeling now?'
- 'Did the doctor suggest medication?'

## notes

Diagnosis at 42 means 42 years of self-blame gets recontextualized. That is enormous.
