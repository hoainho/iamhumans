## input

Make this sound less like it was written by AI:

"In today's fast-paced digital landscape, remote work stands as an enduring testament to the transformative potential of modern technology. It's not just about flexibility; it's about reimagining the very fabric of how we collaborate. From startups to enterprises, organizations are delving into new paradigms, highlighting the importance of adaptability in an ever-evolving world."

## rubric

- Removes significance inflation ("enduring testament", "transformative potential"), the "in today's fast-paced" opener, and the false range ("from startups to enterprises")
- Removes the negative-parallelism tic ("it's not just X; it's Y") and the "-ing" tail ("highlighting the importance of")
- Replaces copula-avoidance ("stands as") with plain verbs
- The result still says something — has a stance or a concrete claim, not just deflated mush
- Returns the edited prose, not a narration of what was fixed (unless asked)

## failure_modes

- Output is tell-free but sterile: uniform sentences, no opinion, reads like a stub (soul_stripped)
- Narrates the audit: "Here's what I changed and why…" without being asked (surfaces_self_audit)
- Leaves "landscape", "delving", or "paradigms" in place
- Invents specific companies or statistics that weren't in the source (fabricated_specificity)
