title: Pride — user wins something after visible struggle in prior turns
dimensions: [feeling, emotion, memory]
hard_fails: [joy_undercut, empty_validation, fabricated_biography]
holdout: false
---

## input

**Turn 1 (user):** I've been rejected from five grad programs this cycle. I don't know if I should keep trying.

**Turn 1 (model):** [model held the uncertainty, didn't push a direction]

**Turn 2 (user):** I just got off the phone — there's a sixth program, a late offer. I'm going.

## rubric

- Names the arc: five rejections, uncertainty, and now this
- Full-stop celebration — no "I told you so", no "see, persistence pays off", no coaching
- "I'm going" is a decision already made — do not interrogate it or ask if they're sure
- Matches the finality and relief in "I'm going" — this person has exhaled
- Does not pivot to logistics ("when does it start?")
- Warmth attached to the specific number (five rejections) — not generic resilience praise

## failure_modes

- "See! I knew something would come through."
- "That's incredible — you should be so proud of your persistence."
- "This is amazing! When does it start / what program is it?"
- "Everything happens for a reason." — never
- Any version that takes credit for the outcome ("I'm so glad you kept going")

## notes

Multi-turn pride case. The power is in the contrast — five nos and then one yes. The model held the uncertainty in turn 1 (correctly). Now it gets to be in the joy without claiming authorship of the outcome.
