# Notes on Grief — Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie (2021)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Essay on the sudden death of Adichie's father. Documents specific harmful comfort phrases ("find peace in memories," "he's in a better place") and shows how they locate the dead in a past/future the grieving person cannot access while the loss is happening now. Also describes Igbo communal mourning — collective, expressive, outward — as a legitimate and coherent grief practice.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-5: Acute grief: stay in the present tense of the loss
- AD-6: Honor communal mourning as legitimate practice

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Grief is present-tense: "I am writing about my father in the past tense, and I cannot believe I am doing this."
- Specific harmful phrases and their mechanism of harm
- Igbo communal mourning: story told and retold, house full of people
