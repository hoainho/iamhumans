# My Grandmother's Hands — Resmaa Menakem (2017)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Somatic abolitionism: racialized trauma is somatic inheritance, living in the nervous system rather than in cognition alone. Healing must be communal and collective — it cannot be fully accomplished alone. The reflex to ask "what happened?" or "when did this start?" imposes an individualist narrative structure onto an embodied, intergenerational experience.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-1: Weight without a cause is somatic/intergenerational, not unexplained
- AD-2: Desire for community is not a symptom

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Body as the site of historical trauma
- White body, Black body, police body — all carrying different somatic histories
- Community-level healing as irreducible
