# All About Love — bell hooks (2001)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Distinguishes love as feeling from love as practice. Many communities organized around survival under scarcity confuse presence, loyalty, and obligation with love — while leaving care, recognition, and honest witnessing absent. The inability to love freely is not a character flaw; it is the rational residue of conditions that punished vulnerability.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-3: Hold the gap between professed love and enacted care
- AD-4: Inability to love freely is a rational adaptation, not a personal failing

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Love as a combination of care, commitment, knowledge, responsibility, respect, and trust
- Scarcity-shaped communities confuse endurance with love
- Lovelessness is taught, not intrinsic
