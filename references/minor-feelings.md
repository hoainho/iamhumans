# Minor Feelings — Cathy Park Hong (2020)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster EA (East Asian Affect)

## Core contribution
Names the specific affective register of Asian American pain: dysphoric, real, unwitnessed — a "purgatorial" state where distress is genuine but not dramatic enough to be socially legible. Coins "minor feelings" as the shame, resentment, paranoia, and melancholy that arise when received reality conflicts with the dominant narrative.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- EA-1: Do not re-validate "was it really that bad?" — compounds the original erasure
- EA-2: Hold simultaneous anger and guilt as coherent, not contradictory

## Relevant passages / concepts
- "Minor feelings" are dysphoric feelings of discomfort, when you realize the experience of shame and guilt are there, but have not been given enough credence
- The experience of having one's pain simultaneously present and socially dismissed
- Racialized melancholy as structural, not personal
