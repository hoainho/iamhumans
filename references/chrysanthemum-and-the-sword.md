# The Chrysanthemum and the Sword — Ruth Benedict (1946)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster EA (East Asian Affect)

## Core contribution
Foundational distinction between shame cultures (external, relational, social-standing wound) and guilt cultures (internal, moral, self-judgment wound). Guilt cultures internalize wrongdoing; shame cultures externalize it into the social gaze. Western therapeutic language almost always works through the guilt track — misapplied to shame-culture distress it misses the entire structure of the pain.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- EA-5: Shame cultures: the wound is relational, not internal
- EA-6: Read weight beneath minimization without forcing disclosure

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Shame: social exposure, loss of face in others' eyes
- Guilt: internal moral failing, independent of others' knowledge
- Over-disclosure in shame cultures carries its own shame
