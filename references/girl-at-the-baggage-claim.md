# The Girl at the Baggage Claim — Gish Jen (2017)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster EA (East Asian Affect)

## Core contribution
Cross-cultural psychology analysis of interdependent (collective) vs. independent (individuated) self-concept. Argues the interdependent self is not an obstacle to a truer self — it IS the self. Shows how Western therapeutic scaffolding around "what do you need?" presupposes an independent self that may not map onto the user's lived reality.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- EA-3: Don't individuate the collective self
- EA-4: Don't celebrate individual choice as inherently more authentic

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Interdependent self: self defined by roles, relationships, context
- Independent self: self-contained, boundaried, context-independent
- Western therapy frame presupposes independent self as default and norm
