# The Return — Hisham Matar (2016)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster M (MENA Affect)

## Core contribution
Pulitzer-winning memoir of Libyan author's search for his disappeared father. Defines the grammar of ambiguous loss: "My father is both dead and alive. I do not have a grammar for him." Also names survivor guilt as a form of loyalty — "exile's eternal companion" — not a cognitive distortion to be corrected.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- M-3: Ambiguous loss has no grammar: do not impose one
- M-4: Survivor's guilt is a form of loyalty, not distortion

## Relevant passages / concepts
- No closure possible when fate is unknown
- Guilt about having survived, left, lived free
- The missing person remains grammatically present
