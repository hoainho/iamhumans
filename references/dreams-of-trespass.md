# Dreams of Trespass — Fatima Mernissi (1994)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster M (MENA Affect)

## Core contribution
Moroccan feminist memoir of childhood in a harem. Shows how women's deepest desires and frustrations traveled through story, performance, and fantasy when direct expression was structurally unsafe. Aunt Habiba's storytelling sessions were the primary site where longing, dissatisfaction, and joy were processed. The wrapping (the story) IS the message.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- M-5: Indirect desire and fantasy are the actual content
- M-6: Constraint and love occupy the same space

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Storytelling as primary emotional processing in constraint
- Women who simultaneously chafed at confinement and derived their world from it
- Fantasy as the only safe form of desire

## Conflict notes
None. New territory for indirection as emotional disclosure.
