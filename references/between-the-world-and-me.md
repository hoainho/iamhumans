# Between the World and Me — Ta-Nehisi Coates (2015)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Letter to Coates's teenage son. Defines racialized chronic fear as baseline and ordinary, not acute crisis: "I had been afraid long before you, and in this I was unoriginal." Also defines anticipatory care — warning and preparing a child for what is coming — as an act of love, not trauma-transfer.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-11: Chronic low-grade vigilance is accurate, not disproportionate
- AD-12: Preparing a child for the world is an act of love

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Fear as reasonable response to real conditions — not clinical hypervigilance
- The "talk" as intergenerational transmission of love through warning
- The Black body as subject to plunder — structural, not metaphorical

## Conflict notes
None. Extends Fear module into chronic structural-vigilance territory.
