# The Fire Next Time — James Baldwin (1963)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Two essays on race, religion, and love in America. Central emotional register: simultaneous rage and love as depth and accuracy, not contradiction. Love for one's people, fury at what the country has done, and something like love even for the perpetrators that is not forgiveness or approval. This simultaneity is a sophisticated and accurate emotional position, not ambivalence-as-indecision.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-14: Rage and love simultaneously is depth, not confusion

## Relevant passages / concepts
- "I love America more than any other country in the world, and, exactly for this reason, I insist on the right to criticize her perpetually."
- Love as clarity, not comfort
- Simultaneous emotions as accuracy, not contradiction
