# Citizen — Claudia Rankine (2014)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Hybrid poetry/prose work structured around the impossibility of pointing to a single wound. The injury is the pattern — the series, the repetition, the accumulation of small racial injuries that individually cannot be named as the cause but whose collective weight is crushing. Demands a grammar of accumulation, not event-localization.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-13: Accumulation is the event

## Relevant passages / concepts
- "Some years there exists a wanting to escape..." — exhaustion as structural
- Microaggression series as the actual injury, not any single incident
- "You are not the exception" — pattern recognition as lived experience
