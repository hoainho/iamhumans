# The Kite Runner — Khaled Hosseini (2003)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster M (MENA Affect)

## Core contribution
Afghan-American narrative of honor, guilt, and multigenerational shame. Amir's guilt is never just personal — it is tied to Baba's honor, the family line, the ethnic community. "There is a way to be good again" (Rahim Khan) is a moral summons requiring action, not a therapeutic invitation to self-forgiveness.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- M-9: Collective shame is real and present
- M-10: Redemption may require action, not reframing

## Relevant passages / concepts
- "There is a way to be good again" — redemption as active project
- Afghan Pashtunwali: nang (honor) and namoos (honor of women) as real structural stakes
- Shame as collective, not individual
