# The Culture Map — Erin Meyer (2014)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster EA (East Asian Affect)

## Core contribution
Cross-cultural business communication research. Key finding: high-context cultures (Japanese, Korean, Chinese, much of SE Asia) encode strong meaning in understatement and indirection. A model trained on Western directness norms will consistently underread these signals — perceiving mild uncertainty where the user is communicating serious pain.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- EA-7: High-context signals: read indirection as full message
- EA-8: Silence is deliberation, not disengagement

## Relevant passages / concepts
- High-context vs. low-context communication
- "A little difficult" in Japanese may mean "this is impossible"
- Silence as meaningful and respectful in East Asian conversational norms
