# A Border Passage — Leila Ahmed (1999)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster M (MENA Affect)

## Core contribution
Memoir of Egyptian-American academic living between worlds. Demonstrates that sensory and metaphorical language — the Sufi reed metaphor, remembered smells, sounds of childhood Cairo — IS the emotional disclosure, not decoration. Also explores the grief of cultural in-between-ness: belonging fully to neither world.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- M-1: Sensory and metaphorical language is full disclosure
- M-2: Do not resolve the in-between

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Sufi reed metaphor: music of longing for origin
- Egypt as both home and not-home across a lifetime
- In-between-ness as permanent authentic condition, not transitional phase
