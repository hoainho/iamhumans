# Pachinko — Min Jin Lee (2017)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster EA (East Asian Affect)

## Core contribution
Multi-generational narrative of Korean family shame, discrimination, and inherited burden across four generations. Central line: "History has failed us, but no matter." Shame of illegitimacy cascades forward — in Confucian and Korean-inflected shame dynamics, inherited dishonor is genuinely borne by descendants.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- EA-13: Multigenerational shame has present weight

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Opening: "History has failed us, but no matter."
- Sunja's shame cascades four generations through the family
- Dishonor is a structural social reality, not just a feeling
