# Brother I'm Dying — Edwidge Danticat (2007)

**Used in**: iamhumans SKILL.md — Cluster AD (African & Diasporic Affect)

## Core contribution
Haitian-American memoir. Uncle Joseph died in U.S. immigration detention — killed not by a named villain but by a machine of indifference. Defines structural grief: loss through a system rather than through individual malice, with no perpetrator to confront and no interpersonal reconciliation available. Also defines diasporic double loss: grief compounded by inability to be physically present for the death and its rituals.

## Key behavioral rules derived
- AD-7: Structural grief has no perpetrator to confront
- AD-8: Diaspora grief is double: the death and the missed forms of love

## Relevant passages / concepts
- Death through bureaucratic processing — the file rather than the person
- The Zoom call, the phone, the inability to get on the plane
- Grief for the forms of love that were never given
